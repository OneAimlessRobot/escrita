imagine this:
An Asian programmer is working,
late at night,
In some code.
either in some kind of personal project
or working for some company.
It doesn't matter too much, though.
And then...
they stumble upon some error:
ok,
let me think of an error here:
for example:
they're trying to connect to the server part of an application
and then...
select() keeps returning immediately 
with a result of -1 (an error)
because...
they,
erroneously,
because they keep trying
to use "-1" as a file descriptor in the fd set variable they are polling with it.
and so...
they will get a runtime message from perror as follows: "invalid argument"
because that would mean
that the maximum file descriptor
would be zero
on the first select call parameter.
so you get invalid argument error.
so then...
they would look at the screen,
at the line of text with the error on it...
everything in that room is quiet for the exception of their CRT monitor,
and the hum of the fan of the computer.
peppered by occasional flickering lights
and hard-disk clicking.
The lights are off,
for the exception of the monitor image washing over their face,
reflecting off of the glasses that they wear.
they also have dark, brown hair
and their skin is of an olive tone.
Dark, almond-shaped eyew
their build is somewhat thin, too.

they stare at it for a while,
slightly leaned forward on their chair,
and at some moment,
they fully recline backwards,
slightly swivling on their office seat

and then...
while squinting their eyes with an inexpressive look that belies a quiet kind of exhaustion,
they quietly mutter to themselves
in in their accent:
"uh...
invalid...
argument."
Keeping the eyes on the monitor for a few moments after,
before realizing the adjustment they needed to make
and resume their work,
after this involuntary break from action.
