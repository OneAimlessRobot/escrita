Name: Bugatti Veyron EB 16.4.
Type: (A *literally* wild) Automobile. A Car. Thats it.

Build: The second example of a hypercar by today's standards that was ever conceived. it began production in 2005. Its body type is classified as "2-Door coupe". The power train is arranged in a mid-engined rear wheel drive fashion, the engine being a 8.0L Volkswagen W16. It is Quad-Turbocharged and it produces a peak power of 987hp. It has capacity for up to 100L of fuel. it is 4.48 meters long, 2 on-the-nose meters wide and 1.2m tall. It tips the scales with a kerb weight of around 2 Tons. The power is mailed to the rear wheels through a 7-Speed automatic DSG gearbox. It became famous after being the first car to ever speed past 400kph

That tells you everything you need to know. Plus... for it to reach its top speed record, it was required to fit it with spec made tyres that come at a cost of around 25k euros per set, because they were devised, not only to take the sheer ammount of load of a two ton hunk of metal going at 400kph possibly in less-than-ideal pavements, but also not completly unsettle the car in case it gets a puncture at such speeds... maintaining composure.
The impressive thing is... when you drive it fast, even at its ludicrous top speeds, it is comfortable, stable, the soundproofing is immaculate, even with such a big engine directly behind your head, and the car does not twitch and does not go out of control. It is gentle and fast. Though it is quad-turbucharged, the power delivery is not abrupt, the DSG transmission is shifts very swiftly and overall it feels like something genuenly made for a biological creature to cruise around at 300kph at the autobahn, not a cyborg to pilot. It is a very surreal, yet fascinating experience. Participants of a survey study designed to assess the opinions of the owners of these vehicles when it came to taking the car from a stand still to its top speed on a very, very high speed, secret, oval test track built by volkswagen in an undisclosed location in germany, have only reported experiencing a gentle, yet clearly noticeable "jerk" upon hitting 200 miles per hour, which just simply corresponded to the Veyron beginning its first phase of engaging in "top speed mode", which involved lowering the suspension and engaging the front diffuser, causing a substantial change in the levels of turbulence from masses of air travelling through and around the car. It is not very nimble around corners, even with technology such as power-steering, TCS, ABS and ASM, due to the sheer ammount of vehicle that needs to be swung around corners. Something to note is, although the car is, clearly, very fast, the features it comes equipped with in base trim are comfortable... even spoiling for what it is. It has:

It makes you wonder: What if they made this... but a 4 door instead of a 2 door coupe? Like... just imagine how well that would sell for multi-millionaires: "Carry your kids to school through the autobahn at 200 mph!"
The body is round shaped, the design of the front bumper gives off a neutral and detached, yet comforting and friendly presence. The headlights are rectangular without slanting or special shaping, the big front grill is practical and the curves are simple. But even then, the overall design feels alive. Not necessarly like it has a personality, but still... alive. It feels organic. The design feels bubblish yet it is visibly shaped for aerodynamic's sake. Overall... it is pretty unpretentious for what it is.
It features an active rear wing that responds in the following ways:
Moving normaly, between 150kph and 375kph - fully raised, low drag blade.
Braking: Fully raised, high drag blade (acts as an airbrake)
Moving normally... below 150kph- Fully lowered.
Moving normally... above 375kph- half raised... low drag blade.
The body has sizeable Ventilation openings in both sides of the cockpit, to feed 2 of the engine radiator grills. The other radiator is in the front. The engine partially exposed, only separated from the exterior via a metallic mesh. it features a rear diffuser and only a Big single exaust exit, which is in the rear.
it also features a dynamic suspension, that lowers to the ground at higher speeds as well as a dynamic front diffuser that reduces drag above 200mph
When it features a fully lowered suspensioned, lowered rear wing and engaged front diffuser, it is in "Top speed mode".




Personality:
Gibongatti Veyronis is a species of automobilles with only a single population hotspot somewhere in germany. They roam the cities in large schools, often in roaming clusters of around 35-40. They are peaceful creatures which prefer to run away when provoked than to cause a commotion. They enjoy going fast and stick to the autobahn, where they are actually several times less likely per unit of vehicle than either BMWs or Audis to cause crashes out of spite or fear. These gregarious creatures feel the most at ease while cruising in a spear-like formation, with one Male Gibongatti, often the one considered the healthiest in the group, at the front, responsible for towing the rest of the pack while pushing through upwards of 30 Thousand Liters of Air per minute, due to its large frame and big air-cooling requirements for its powerful 8.0L heart at around 200 Miles an hour of sheer wind resistance, making the job easier for the females, that often travel in the core of the pack to protect the children. The weaker males go in the outer shell of the travelling formation, taking occasional chances to overtake the ones in front throughout their migrations, in order to attract the attention of the females and respect from other competing males. Their migrations have a simple destination: any gas stations that they manage to find along the autobahn that spans all around germany. They are not considered an endangered species and their population numbers have always remained relatively stable throughout their existence since 2005.

They cannot resist the company of another Veyronis automobile. While at the gas stations, they banter, males and females alike scrape bumpers and panels and males have a rare chance to let go of their rivalries and enjoy a good laugh while pushing eachother.

During the migration, single-make drag races, while the group is in a 3 wide by n long formation, the more time a male spends in a position close to the front, the higher are its chances to procreate and spread its genes to the following generation of Gibonatti Veyronises.

In Portugal, as first catalogued and classified by car-zoologist Adriano António Campos Valente, (its me bitch!), They are also called: "Bugate Veirão" for singular or "Bugattis Veirões" for plural.

They work in synchronising gearshifts between group members, so no one gets left behind.

They communicate through heavier or softer engine revving, using context and the surrounding environment to decypher the needs of eachother

The females also have their hands full while at the pack, as the female who, while still within the core of the pack, is the highest in the forward-pointing order, is the one that gets to mate with the strongest and most vigorous Male Gibonatties.


They tend to avoid tight, twisty roads... and younger specimen will only get to learn to adapt to such places either out of sheer necessity from losing contact with their original group or having suffered a wreck from a careless BMW, Audi or Paul Walker.

They do not enjoy the summer, as they tend to struggle with cooling.
But also not the apex of winter, as they tend to struggle with grip and pack racing gets more risky.

Bugattis Veirões that end up without a pack or grow in captivity tend to suffer from invertility and often grow aimless and low-mooded. There are no registered anecdotes of Gibonattis being sucessively re-introduced in the Autobahn after accidentally leaving the pack, ceasing to recognize their own kind after just 1 week in isolation and becoming completly vegetative after a month.

Veyronattis recognize their own kind, but not really individuals. It has even been theorized that, if one Bugati leaves their own school and joins the slipstream of another mid-migration, neither member of the group will acknowledge, either the missing member or the new member.

Veyronatti schools stuck in slow or highly congested traffic that forces the group to split up, including all the higher order males and females, feel extremely uneasy and, specially in very slippery or bumpy roads, may lose control and become lost... taking several tens of minutes to reverse and reorient themselves towards the destination, after which they wait until they hear the sound of another Veyronatti school... timing their throttling just right so that they are able to match the speed of the oncomming train, immediatly shifting towards the end of the pack, where they join the other lower order males and find themselves having to climb up the ranks once again.
