Shibuya Incident: the aftermath.

Maki Zenin,
Still reeling from everything that happened,
Just stood there
Admiring her own handywork,
The horror.
Unspoken boundaries
Broken.
Fantasies of revenge
Enacted.
It all felt so cathardic
But now...
Its all over.
Yuji has fallen from grace
And Sukuna has gotten its piece of the pie
It destroyed life
And left it alone to rot to completion

But...
From the rubble,
A curse.
The herbal predator spawns out of thin air.
There was no tell,
No gust of wind
Or even a sound.

She materializes as quickly as one can turn their heads
And see them.

Witnessing Maki zenin's sunken body language.
The absense of thought
The buried,
Trudging emotion that just
Wont
Go
Away

She approaches the blood stained sorcerer
Saying this,
In rather narcissistic third person speech:

"...
From the grief
And from the ash
A new curse has emerged:
Flora: The Herbal Predator
The fear of trying again.
"

"
The seed will have to bear the strongest of the storm
On its frailest leaves.
It will take time to mature
And thicken.
And you have more years in front of you.
The sooner you let emotion fully set in
Without lashing out
The sooner you heal
the sooner you forget
"



And then,
She goes quiet.

From the bottom of her sole,
A branch slithers its way out
Beginning to sprawl across the ground beneath.
Into the cracks in concrete
Thin ramifications infiltrate,
Searching for soil.

The curse just stands there
Blankly staring while all of thise goes on.
It is growing.
Growing from your grief.
And from the charred soil and ash under the destroyed city.
Fire kills life
Life is born from ash as the roots thicken,
Spreading further and further in.
Capilary in thinness,
Now even the tiniest roots resemble pencils
Still flexible and green
But pulsating with life.

In the exposed skin...
In her arms,
underneath her dress
In her face
In her colarbone of the grieven curse,
To the rythm of sun beams shifting,
Following its whims,
Leaves grow from gracefully cyclic openings in her skin,
Flaring out just before they sink back down,
Leaving behind just the tiniest of pores.
These get replaced by new ones
And it repeats.

And then...
Suddenly,
Flora's roots begin to break down the concrete below
Reducing it to tiny crums
That expose the soil below like her shadow.
From her abdomen
And her back,
Thick,
Wooden branches begin to cage the predator inside of this cavity she has created
Armoring herself in a mesh of plant,
Bush
And soil..
Anchored to the ground,
As still as the wind allows

Flora,
The curse of grief,
is here to stay.

And she will grow
For as long as people refuse to forget.
