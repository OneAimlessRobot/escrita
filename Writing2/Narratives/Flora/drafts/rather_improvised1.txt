Title: "Rather improvised"




"I am Flora, the grass knot priestess of twigs and bunches
The flowers bloom when I take steps across the garden
Tipping grass blades
constricting them with my toes
and squashing the soil with the soles of my feet.
The mud is squishy, the grass is wet
The sky becomes sad when it becomes too cold
it cries, coating the crust
with the sweat from its eyes.
I am Flora, the humble ambassador of plants, people
girls, Men and everything in between.
...
Just not fungi. I think they are weird...
But if you’re either a plant
or an animal... or an insect.
Insects are animals too, didn’t you know?

I don’t remember what I was talking about.

...

The sun balms the sore and the grieving with its calm beams
leading them through messy, soaked afternoons after school
when everybody wants to go home
and has no time for a friendly hug. When we embrace nature, weather stops being circumstance
and starts being like the mood. Or even humor! Isn't it funny when rain starts right after you get your hair done! No one predicted that on TV did they? The sky was plain blue just a few moments ago
But the humid sobbing from the sky has decided to ruin your day just because. Probably someone left them upset,
or angry, or their eye hurts from too much time in class reading things from projectors!
I know I hated that when I was still in school.
But now Flor the monarch of daisies and orchids needs to Flock to some other place
As her mood has gotten sour from standing in the shade for too long
and craves the gentle reminder that, no matter for how long the clouds cover the sky, it has always preceded sunny days and happier times
