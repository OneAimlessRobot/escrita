Transformation example:


Let's assume that Flora is just sitting in the middle of the street
But that there is an adequately thick layer of soil underneath the concrete and asphalt:
First, Flora will summon a moderately thick root stem from one (Or both of) her heel (heels)
And stand very still.
This stem will then recursively split into smaller terminals until she feels confident about her ability to slither them into the inside of the concrete. These tiny split roots will slowly ooze their way through using a potent acid (If Flora had not developed an ability to ooze acid, she would  not be able to do this
And would require an ample patch of thick soil to do this on)
Which will gradually widen the gaps
Allowing successively thicker parts of the root to grow deeper into the ground and continue. This process can cost a lot of nutrients
So she will stop immediately once some soil is found.
Once the soil is found,
She has to start feeding herself immediately.
The roots grow stronger, thicker
And this thickening will propagate upwards,
eventually result in a wooden shielding forming.
Their hold on the soil will also get progressively firmer and firmer.
Once the roots grow strong enough,
Flora will become very hard to move from her spot
And a violent shove, for example, will merely make her body tilt backwards at speed
Only to come back up (And make Flora very dizzy).
Eventually,
The roots will have worn out the concrete
Until it becomes frail
And the only thing holding the entire structure together is Flora's connection to the roots
Which grip onto the soil
Feeding the seed that she has on her gut.
Once the concrete is too weak, however,
It will CRUMBLE, forming a potentially very big hole
Onto which Flora will fall
To then get her fall safely dampened by her roots
At the base of the cavity. These cavities have the shape of approximate, roughed-out semi-spheres.
Once Flora is in the center of the cavity,
The roots will VIOLENTLY, at her own command,
Bury her alive! Right under the soil.
A HUGE seed in her gut, that has been getting fed this entire time,
Will quickly begin devouring Flora's original body
Integrating it in the memory of the seed
To get built once it is over.
A SPRAWLING network of thick Logs will EXPLODE out of her body
Ravaging any soil in their path. Flora is now in Sprawling Garden form: An autonomous, mobile, semi-sentient network of branches
That, growing from the big seed, dig underground while lugging the it along.
The entire structure is basically a intertwined complex of both roots and Commons
And these roots will be the network's only nutrient intake.
This seed is very, very tough
And fire will not destroy it. The branches will quickly regenerate if cut
But "Flora" becomes effectively blind
And only aware of her surroundings through vibrations in the ground as well as sound.
Flora can be communicated with
but wont talk back.
Flora will regularly SPIKE through the ground with several thick log terminals
To spread nasty spores and other chemicals into the air
And incapacitate opponents.
However,
if an opponent has not moved in a while,
The structure will gradually creep up on them
And SUDDENLY hold them down into the ground. Then, she may pierce them with needles
And open cuts, and bruises in the skin
Until larger intrusions are able to penetrate.
Flora will use anti-coagulants to prevent scarring,
Insert roots inside of the opponent...
And then slowly, but surely consume them from the inside
Leaving, at best, only some of the skeleton behind.
The structure can tunnel underground at up to 30 kilometers per hour,
Which causes noticeable shaking as well as deafening noise.
