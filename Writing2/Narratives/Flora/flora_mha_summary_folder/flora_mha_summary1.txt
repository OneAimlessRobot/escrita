Flora de Vaght (Unemployed Ex. Hero Student)

RP entry

(This is the summary of loads of lore)

Flora de Vaght is a 22 year old girl
She is 158 centimeters tall
And weighs 57 kilograms.
Has grey eyes,
short shoulder-level
Straight blue hair.
Pale white skin
And a birth sign on her lower back.
Her voice is soft and gentle
And she barely talks.
Her demeanor is alert and anxious when in public
But in private
She appears very relaxed
Almost to the point of complacency.
Currently unemployed,
She wanders around Japan
Mostly on foot
Running various errands for locals
In order to be able to sleep on their house for the day.

When rejected,
She sleeps on forest soil
Using her quirk to bury herself on the dirt
Away from the elements.

She has not seen her parents in years
As Flora ran away
Overwhelmed with all of the expectation
To "behave normally"

(She decided
After being allocated to train with Endeavor
That one meeting was enough
And that being a hero was not for her)

Flora has very limited basic fitness
As she runs slowly
Cannot jump very high
And runs out of breath very quickly.
She is the opposite of picky with her food
And will eat virtually anything that fits inside of her mouth
And sometimes
Even what does not fit.

Flora is neurodivergent
Being diagnosed as being on the spectrum
Since age 4.
Her mother is a psychotherapist
And gives her daughter anger management appointments
As Flora tends to experience violent meltdowns
From the fact that she has very,
Very poor emotional regulation
And every little annoyance
And grievance that she experiences
Will "pass straight through" to her core.

Flora's external apathy and detachment
Belie her main coping mechanism
Which is to vent about her various woes through an "inner child"
With a sassy, petty, self important personality.
Flora is not aware of these rants
And is incapable of occasionally letting one come out

Flora's favourite subjects are:
- Biology
- Chemistry
...
- She can tolerate philosophy
And that is about it.
Flora has incomprehensible struggle with virtually anything else
Specially logical/mathematical tasks.

Flora is enrolled in the heroes course
But in the branch of special needs
As
Despite being almost at college level in her Naturalist/chemistry reasoning
Her mathematics are below highschool level.

On her first day,
She got the room where she was supposed to study wrong
And ended up melting down in class
As the frustration from not being able to keep up got to her.

She broke a chair
And spent 5 whole minutes screaming
And slamming a table with her fists.
When everyone went to the classroom to see what was happening,
The math teacher from special needs saw her
And everything was explained.

However,
Flora had to be restrained
And it took Midnight,
Endeavor
AND Aizawa to do it
As Aizawa restrained her powerful quirk
While Endeavor threatened to call her parents
As Midnight worked on putting her to sleep.

It was not a very good first day.

In special needs class,
Everyone is very cooperative with each other
And Flora is not discriminated against
As this is My Hero Academia
A show where the emotional appeal
Is on rejecting ableist narratives.

Flora's quirk has her name.
"Flora".
She can grow branches 
That emerge directly from openings in her skin
Which can also retract.
In these branches,
Flora can summon leaves
Which she is almost perpetually covered by
As she requires sunlight to stay awake
Which means that being in class
Is very hard for her at any time that is not the summer.
Flora can create tolerance to any biological substance
Even if she gets sick at 1st
She has ingested many things
Such as m****uana,
Botox
She is able to cast plants in her branches
That produce those substances.
Flora's communication style is very literal,
She has trouble keeping eye contact
And has very magical thinking
When it comes to interpreting social rules.
"Oh.
But I thought that only other people needed to cry..."
During her meltdowns,
She can cast thick clouds of poison
That require evacuation.
This is part one.
I hope that it is enough.
She is hetero
Plant/human gender - M/F
