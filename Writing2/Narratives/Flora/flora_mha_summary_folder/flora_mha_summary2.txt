Quirk description

The quirk is called "Flora". The quirk completely restructures the anatomy of the user, making them dependent on two separate ways of obtaining nutrients: A plant based digestive system
Which is meant to turn inorganic materials,
Like minerals, into organic compounds
And an animal based digestive system.
Which is meant to turn organic materials
Back into inorganic.
Flora's powers grow from organic material reserves that she builds up based on what she eats and are used to maintain a wooden chassis structure
That runs alongside her skeleton and allows Flora users to, after opening orifices in the skin, which give us a peek into layers as deep as the skeleton
(They also ooze a protective liquid that prevents abrasion
And temperature shock for the small amount of time that the orifice is  opened)
grow stems that can vary in thickness, hardness, flexibility
And can be of two types:
1 - Common
2 - root.
Common stems are called vines, when flexible and not protected by wood, branches when they are covered by wood, but still rather thin and logs,
when both thick AND protected by wood.
Root stems can only grow from places in the body
Located at or below the colon
But will NOT sprout from the hips, pelvis, thighs, shins or knees.
Common stems cannot grow from places covered by clothing.
Flora (the person) prefers growing these from her foot heels, as the skin there is thick
And won't give her the annoying chill sensation that comes from the temporary opening in the skin.
(She describes them as being about as annoying
Like drinking cold water after eating a meal which was way too hot.
"It makes my skin ring in a crappy way."
But common ones can grow from any part that does not include the face, though.
Flora technically has two brains, as well.
Where the first one is the "1+1=2", "Killing is wrong" kind and the second is a very rudimentary one that constantly works on figuring out how to autonomously grow commons through exposed skin, develop one or two leaves
And then return back to the inside of her body to be recycled and give place to another small branch. In sunny days, this process is specially active,
And she is often covered in small stems that slowly retreat...
And flare out. Spring up a single leaf and sink back down in a pulsing rhythm.
If the sun shines on her head, she will, indeed, develop flowers there.
Flora can also perform this voluntarily
Just like one is able to take conscious control of their breathing.
Flora users have abysmal night vision and terrible with directions
And their biology grants them very bad base fitness, stamina and explosive power outside of their quirk. Flora (The person) has a very bad sense of balance as well
And can easily be tripped and feinted past in Basketball.
The flowers that Flora is able to grow depend on which ones she has eaten. And her body allows her to synthesize hybrid species depending on the situation. Flora users have differences in the potential for the scale of certain actions,
Like the toughness of their logs, how quickly they form branches and how many pollen spores they can summon relative to the amount of flowers they have deployed.
Flora is [extremely] talented at spawning
[Truck loads] of spores,
Of very ample variety, as well
The effects of her spores include:
1- Paralysis (Involuntary, relentless muscle contraction)
2- anesthetic (Sleep/drowsiness)
3- Poisonous (irritant/Infectant).

Flora is also able to cover her branches in microscopic needles
through which she can inject any compound that she knows about
(It currently includes thc)

Flora can transform.
