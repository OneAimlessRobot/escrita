```
Burning slowly.

The wind that wails in the forest fills me up
producing echoing sounds of an empty bottle
where meaning and struggle used to live.
Thrown around like a banana peel after lunch
I stay innocent so that I never learn.

Whatever can I control
if I was made this way?
Addictions are my friends
because they dont abandon me.

We share this house of turmoil together
finding solace in the mess
making peace with ourselves.

I dont know how to care, anymore.
i am in too deep.
I feel lonely
but I feel free

Bushes rustle
leaves swirl around
as I walk through this dark,
dark forest
until the sun rises at the horizon.

When the first rays perk out
I must forget the beauty they carry.
Taking shelter under the ground
until the next night comes by.

I am agnostic to nature
indifferent morally.
you cannot hurt me
because I dont let you.

If they scream,
I quiet myself
and if they swing
I turn away
and leave.

But just as I shield myself from the mess inside,

I always ask:
could someone,
anyone drag me out alive,
with my youth still attached

And do it
before I drown?


I had 19 years to write this
as my body remained still in the cycle of life.
Artificial
blood from the depths of hell hoists me to the masses like a trophy.

Im getting used to being a monster.
I was born a warrior
and staying sane is my battle


Eventually,
I wont be 30, anymore

When will I drop the act of innocence
and pretend I am new?
i am a lady.
A woman of dignity and respect
with real adult issues to take care of
without fuss
without thrashing.

They feed me the veggies of responsibility
but I spit them all out in spite.

Stuck
but aching...

Thinking about choice
leaves me so...

Paralized


```
