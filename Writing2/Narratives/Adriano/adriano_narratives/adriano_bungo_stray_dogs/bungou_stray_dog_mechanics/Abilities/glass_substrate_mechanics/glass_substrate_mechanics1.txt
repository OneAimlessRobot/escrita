glass_substrate_mechanics1

This is the beginning:
Its a live demonstration of a situation where the ability would be used
in the context of Bungo Stray Dogs,
which is followed by a comprehensive intrduction of how it works.

[After I run back to cover,
I go behind the container,
hidden.
I actually have not one,
but three (actual)
abilities.
but one of them only works when I expose myself to my own milkhaze,
as it requires me to be dead calm to use:

Glass Substrate.

This ability allows me to become invisible under geometrically rigid constraints:

Upon activating my ability,
I will become completely camoflaged
from a line of view
which is directed by a vector perpendicular to said surface.
The requirement is:
In whatever surface I mesh my back to
(whether it is laying on the floor,
leaning on a post,
leaning on a wall, etc.)
the whole section of spine that goes from just under the shoulder blades
all the way to the tail bone has to be in contact with said surface.
It makes it impossible for me to peek behind corners,
make ambushes without compromising my camouflage before striking,
(not important.
Im not a fighter, anyway.
Im more of a status effect supporter)

and if,
while in camouflage,
the surface is coated in highly irregular patterns with highly contrasting colors,
like black and white stripes
or green spirals with a pink background
or something like that,
I can easily be spotted if people look at me from the side,
as the patterns distort from those angles.
Someone just not really paying high amounts off attention
will have a much, much lower chance of spotting me,
but I also need to try,
whenever possible,
to make sure that the surface I choose is as plain as possible
(for example:
a plain red surface would make me invisible from virtually any angle.
)

Also:
while camouflaged,

Glass Substrate also overrides
the minute shadings formed by wrinkles in the user's clothing.
so those will not matter

also...

I may let out a startled gasp
if something scares or surprises me,
which,
aside from revealing my position with sound,
which will still be difficult to pinpoint.
Or...
It may also make me lose the camouflage entirely
because I lose my cool.
But...
I wont lose the camouflage from a small spike in stress.
I need to feel something like a knife suddenly being thrown in my direction
so that the camouflage stops.
If something like a harmless fruit fly lands on me,
Or a colleague is by my side and touches me
It wont make a difference on the camouflage.

essentially:
I dont need to be Monk-level peaceful...
But if I feel like my life is truly in danger,
It will not activate until my levels of emotional tension lower again.

Also...
anything that touches me,
if it is in solid form,
will still reveal my position.
if a bunch of ants crawl all around my body while I am camouflaged,
they will reveal the shape of my body.
(Gelatine will not coat me with the color of the solvent,
but will mildly coat me with the color of the solute,
the color deepening with the concentration of the jello powder.
Paint will reveal me,
As it is not technically a liquid.
Instead being a mixture with a solvent,
Some additives and,
most importantly,
A fine smearing of pigment)
The color of the paint will only reveal itself
once Glass Substrate is no longer in effect.
and I will look dirty once I am visible again.

Oh.
And even in camouflage,
the bulging feet are a problem
In case the surface which my feet lay on
has a different color and/or pattern
from the surface which my back is laid on.

Notes:

A creative liberty I chose to iron out
is the fact that,
in real life,
even if fully leaning on a surface,
Your spine will never fully touch a wall,
only the area of the tail bone
And area around the shoulder blades.
But Bungo Stray Dogs,
the universe that this is made for,
Is a cartoon.
So I will assume that all characters there,
including my OC,
can ALL fully plant their backs on solid surfaces with no effort,
even if that is unrealistic,
as it would require their spines to suddenly get slightly longer for that to happen and stuff...]
