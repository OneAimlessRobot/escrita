cloud_needle_mechanics1

Its simple:
I have too many "status condition" powers.
I wanted something I could actually attack with.

What I am looking for,
I guess Ill just have to explain it,
Is a power called Cloud Needles.
Its an AoE style power which lets me cast 
stormy clouds of sharp hail.
These clouds,
Once spawned,
Will then compress and densify indefinitely,
Until thunder begins to form.
the wind will then begin to pick up,
Beginning to make the clouds move
And, therefore,
To fall by themselves as sharp shards of ice.
These shards will be small,
At most as big as the needles from a porcupine.
The issue is that these needles will be made of billions of them
And will be propelled by relentless,
Winds which rage at about a hundred miles per hour.
They will likely touch 
Every square meter of floor area
In a diameter of one kilometer around the user that casts it.

it is driven by overwhelming feelings of emotional exhaustion,
Post-nightmare-like bitterness and irritability,
And accumulated pressure from bureaucratic neglect.

it will be instinctively enabled by the user
Once he gets angry enough.
Once he is put to the side enough.
Unfairly challenged or questioned enough.
And it is a really rare state for Adriano to reach.
So he does not really count it as an ability.

He cannot really "use" cloud needles,
As it is very instinctual.
it could be useful in combat,
But the clouds only appear
Not just when Adriano is cornered,
As he can still turn into a mouse through
"Deathless Rat",
But when he is really,
[Really] cornered for reasons that are both:
1- unfair
2- outside of his control.
Relieving him from the situation
will make the display stop immediately

And Adriano will then collapse into the ground,
Fainted from spiritual fatigue.

As the ability progresses,
Adriano will begin rehearse
Delirious expressions of violence
Which increment in intensity every few seconds.

"Scab their shins..."
"Bite their ears"
"Break their ribs"
"Sample their muscles"
"Grind their intenstines"
...
...
...

"Bodies everywhere"
"Bodies everywhere"
"Bodies everywhere"

The facial expression Adriano makes
is one of disgust and contempt.
At others
At himself,
His look bent, distorted 
In a straight up cartoon-like way
He will point at others a lot.
Even when they are allies when talking to them,
Predominantly screech and scream
With almost no clarity behind it whatsoever
Just enough to keep the raging 
demolition job running.
His eyes are a mix of yellowed out and bloodshot,
His mouth and brows move in erratic, jagged ways,
His voice cracks very frequently
Which,
Aside from possibly multiple cuts around his limbs, torso and face,
In case he isnt protected adequately,
He is also left with a sore throat after the move

These needles are the side of porcupine spikes
But move at one hundred miles per hour,
There are at least thousands of them 
For every 1 cubic meter of air
That you sample during this "power".

Before the ability starts,
Queues for activation include:
1- Sky gets very, very grey.
2- winds begin to pick up sharply
over the course of a couple minutes 
3- Adriano will begin to grunt and clench his fists 
in generalized, unchanneled frustration and disgust
Directed at the immediate environment that surrounds him

This ability is a bit like if the Sandstorm weather in Pokemon
existed as an attack instead,
it was ice type
Oh wait!
it does!
yeah. its like hail storm (I think its called like that?)

This ability is like the "Hail" move in Pokemon.
Except the Pokemon that uses it
Faints automatically after it is over
As well as getting damage from it 
while the move is running.


It has the same activation rules as Milk Haze:
it cannot be activated from a stuttered attempt,
It cannot be activated mid sentence,

Where a "sentence"
is defined by the internal speech intentions of the user.
if the user is tired and says: "I used... hah... milk haze",
That pause between "used" and "milk" will not make it a new sentence.
The user has to [mean] it.
As in:
"milk haze".
Just saying it.
The same is for "cloud needles".

Also...
The symbolism is the following:
While "milk haze" is a metaphor for waking up relieved and well-rested,
Filled with tolerant, morning-time wisdom,
Where your willpower and emotional control are at maximum,

"Cloud needles" is triggered by the opposite:
A power nap after a long night of studying before an exam,
Complimented by industrial doses of machine coffee once the student arrives at college,
Or someone isolated,
neurotic and lonely waking up crying 
and sweating after a nightmare.
Or after a night where 
one [only] fell asleep 
after a hour-long screaming session 
about "Them" 
and "why only me".

The person feels bleached inside,
they have no capacity for emotional tone modulation,
And their words appear to always be either:
Painfully bitter
Or desperately ached.


Adriano will also swear...
A lot during this state of mind,
Experiencing sharp spikes and drops 
of blood flow to his brain,
Making him feel unbearable headaches,
that potentially drive him to his knees,
If the storm is to go on 
for too long without a break.

The internal forces 
that chaotically string him around
And drive the storm will,
eventually,
Completely deplete him of energy 
and strength.

But it won't cease.

In a terminal stage,
Adriano may also begin 
to cry profusely,
Refusing to get up,
Clinching at his gut
Unrecognizable now being 
the internal world even to him,
Shattered having been all mirrors
been let out all screams having been, as well...
Only with tears of grief 
over an erased identity...
Remaining.
