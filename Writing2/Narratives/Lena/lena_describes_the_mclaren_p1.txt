lena_describes_the_mclaren_p1


It screams fire dressed in dark, purple velvet.
it feeds itself from organic waste,
And it feeds itself from what sensations are made of.
The gasoline burning spins the rear wheels,
The wheels are spun by a war chest full of angry-grieved wolves they call an engine,
Which is enhanced by the electric motors,
The body...
Carved by artists driven by the lust the Bible warns us off...
While channeling the precision Athena is praised for.
Hunting for speed in her quiet celestial realm,
The throttle...
a rail cannon which drives you 2 seconds into the future every time you floor it.
The pavement seemingly curling and bending like cheap carpet at any speed below 60 miles an hour.
The cornering.
The glue of vacum fed to the bottom of the car by diffusers,
And the ugly witchery performed by the wizards in lab coats.
Their spell books being the laptops they wield at the wind tunnels.
The rims, jet black.
The windshield so open...
So exposed...
So raw...
it is Great Britain if it had to scream every atrocity it ever commited.
And it feels damn justified.
The British already admitted they were wrong.
They had already said they were sorry.
And the McLaren P1 is the answer to those that wont stop mocking,
Won't stop salting their wounds...
Those who won't stop...
Mocking their corrupt monarchs,
Which they already hated back in the 80s,
The horrible city design,
Straight out of a call of duty game based in Pripyat.
The self deprecation which they cannot stop uttering,
But everyone seems to ignore,
That sometimes...
The most outwardly composed gentlemen...
Are the most rotten fiends in disguise.
Everyone hates the British,
The British hate themselves.
And under that compression?
A flare of unyielding,
Explosive brilliance comes out.

It is the Mclaren P1: The Widowmaker.

You step on it...
And it lunges.

if you dare not flinch?

It bites back.

A gash in the shape of colonial guilt,
Blood made from the money from innocent taxpayers,
Unspoken political atrocities,
Depravity,
Royal nepotism,
Unearned impunity...

And when it explodes...
The english,
the Scottish...
All fighters deep down?
They bite back in the only way they know how:
Precision...
A daring sense of innovation and inquisitiveness...

And the madness of hundreds of minds who never stop pushing beyond what is deemed "Sane" or "appropriate".

For all I care?

I agree.

"Measured" people can go suck on milk...

[Sigh...]

What a damn cool ass car.
