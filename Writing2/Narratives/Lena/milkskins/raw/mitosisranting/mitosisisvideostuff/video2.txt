except for a glue potion and optionally an evolution
And specially if they have a name in the format:
Player[big number goes here]...
And if you are used to playing *with* gear...

1- you will have a bad day against them, as they are absolute gremlin goblins and if they catch you lacking, will go after you until they are absolutely sure you cannot strike back.
2- You will get gluesplit/popsplit/whatever
Anyways)
And I think it was the same for Orbb, too.
The split distance, the speed, the bonuses...
When you play this game for long enough those aids just bore into your muscle memory and make it difficult to re-adjust to playing without gear again.
Another thing: The timing for the glue split trick also depends on what gear you use
(Stats such as: The velocity your cells travel at while you are re-joining
(re-join speed)
Your split speed
Your *actual* speed)
But Orbb was also under the same constraints so that does not matter,
Although, the duel could have also been decided from how flexible you are about that change in rythm.
Another thing to point out is that, as previously mentioned, I was not taking it very seriously and doing everything I could to win.
As I was recording, 
I went for a cheeky/flashier approach 
in trying to get a gluesplit on Orbb... 
but that is a massive disadvantage, 
as gluesplits are not easy to do consistently for the average player 
(from the players I have met/that I know of)
While Orbb, actually taking it seriously, used reliable potions, 
like the shield, liberally and timedly.
The fact that I could not perform my beloved "split plus feed" trick using the machinegun-like feed speed provided by the legendary starter gear really tripped me up and,
As I am neurodivergent and really sensitive to small changes,
Those things kept lingering me throughout the duel.
Also... something that always trips me up in this is:
When does one claim that they have won a mitos.is skirmish?

Is it when they rage quit.
I dont really know...
But what matters, is that they won...
under the same constraints.
