Okay so this is a new day:

I just came up with a way to categorise players 
in terms of their temperament inside a Mitos.is Lobby.
I think Ill make it a multi-dichotomy system with the axis
1- Aggressive axis
2- The agreeable axis
3- The expressive axis
4- Individualistic axis
5- IRL money spender axis
These are the static axis
4- The chaotic axis
5- The flexible axis
And these are the dynamic axis

The values are all real numbers which range from -100 to 100


the Aggressiveness Axis essentially measures how high 
the tendency of a player to want to engage in Celular Combat (TM) is.
How much their discourse is focused on PvP in general 
as opposed to real life events or in-game social happenings
(How much they like fighting)

The agreable axis measures the tendency of a player to
seek resolving social conflicts, either among all players in the server 
or as a mediator between others
How curious they are about new players, 
plus how proactive they are in trying to integrate them with the rest of the group
How quickly they begin trusting new players
how difficult it is to break their trust
(How much they seek social harmony)

the expressive axis measures how vocal, outspoken or socially open
a player is while logged in.
How much they send chat messages
How much they abanter
How vocal they are about the events within the server/other players
How much time they spend inventing unorthodox ways to engage with the game
(How socially outgoing they are)

the individualistic axis measures how willing some player is to engage in collaboration with others 
when they need to engage in combat
For example...
If they struggle to defeat a player, 
and are highly individualistic, 
they might find it hard to set their own pride aside in order to engage that one player
Also measures how willing they are to ask questions directly/ask for help with farming xp, 
training a skill, etc.
They are not necessarily unexpressive.
Or lacking in agreableness
Sometimes, when players leave and are high on the scoreboard, 
they may prompt other players: 
"Hey ppl? Who wants my mass? Im leaving."
The individualistic player will rarely want their mass, 
prefering to earn it on their own instead.
if they are prompted directly, they will often turn the offer down, 
even if the mass they are offered may help them with xp.

5- the money spender axis is pretty simple:
Zero is neutral about spending money
High means they fundamentally feel like they cannot play/enjoy the game without spending money
And negative means they explicitly refuse spending money in game
Correlates rather high with individualistic players
And oddly low with not-so individualistic players

The chaotic axis simply measures the volatility with which the static traits change over time
driven by instinct/invisible internal drive
So basically... how often and erratically a player changes their static axis based on instinct, 
feeling or internal, unexpressed values/logic


And the flexible axis measures how willing a player is to change their static axis *consciously*
in order to fit (according to their own principles) the situation at hand more adequately.
