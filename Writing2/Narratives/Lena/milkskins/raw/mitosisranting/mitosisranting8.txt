I think I have a new way of defining the ethics of "targeting" in Mitos.is:
It will be considered "targeting" if the mass factor between the bigger player and the smaller player is above 8 towards the side of the bigger.
I say 8 because, in this game,
you can split into a maximum on 16 cells.
So each cell will be a 16th of your total size.
So if you are, at least, 1/8th of their size... it means that,
Even in the presence of resilience...
You *always* have a chance of fighting back against your opponents,
as you can *guarantee* that you can eat one of their 16 cells
(You are as big as two of those)
And it guarantees that, if you are clever and resourceful...
mathematically...
you can *always* defeat your opponent in theory.
So yeah...
Ask your opponent their mass...
And if they are under 1/8th of your size...
leave them alone
