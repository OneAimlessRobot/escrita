[Lena was in Yokohama
Walking along a river.
She was skipping class
having taken an underground train to be here.

She did not feel the need to do anything.
Being in class was annoying.
Writing poetry,
Which she did as a hobby, was annoying. 

Her mind was filled with static. And nothing around her brought arousal.
The weather featured a dull mix of chill air,
Cloudy sky and high humidity
Which made her feel cold no matter what she wore.
Just not the kind that would compel her to go back home
And just lay in bed for the whole day.

While walking, she noticed something interesting.
One of the bridges was opening up
To give way to a large container ship.
The colorful, branded huge boxes stimulated her aesthetic appetite for standardized sizes.
The boxes were all the same size.
It did not matter if a single box contained a passenger vehicle
or 7 million spice bags. It could be anything!
This pondering kept her entertained for a while
But then the ship had fully gone under the bridge
And would soon get too far away to find any details.
She did notice, however, that one of the boxes was about to fall to the river.

"Surely someone will handle it."
She thought.

More walking ensued.
More boredom
More internal, petulant complaining about how the weather is annoying.
Which is the precise moment where she thinks: "Alright. I'm gonna buy something good"

Lena forgets her lingering grief about the disappearing ship
And went to buy frozen lasagna from the convenience store.
She buys the equivalent of 6 meals of the stuff
Which she can typically eat in two days.

After spending a grand total of zero minutes in school, this far
She arrives home, Locks the door and every single shutter in every window

(It was 3PM)

And goes to the kitchen. Lena heats up a bowl of soup from the social store
As being an orphan gives her right to subsidies and many kinds of aids until she is age 25
And finally sits down.

"[Sigh...........]
I am so glad that I am not in the military anymore. Those girls in CQC sparring were TOTAL creeps.
And the officers were annoying, too!
"Stop crying!
we WILL turn you into a woman with CHEST.
Now.
Get back up and take her down!"
After drills,
They (her drill-mate girls) would always surround me when the officers had left
And call me names, and make me feel bad.
So they can go $%&$ themselves."

Lena was already fed up with the meal
So she slurps the entire bowl of soup down in seconds
And hurls the empty container at the bin
Completely missing the target
And leaving droplets of soup smeared all over the previously clean floor
Which would surely dry out
And look gross every time she walks in here.

But anyways.
She is about to go to the best part: The Lasagna!
The uniform, pasty, easy-to-chew Lasagna that she has been eating on every meal which is not breakfast
For the past... half of a year.

She had to find some distraction from the fact that her life felt like an endless
bizarre anxiety nightmare
Where she was a 21 year old girl hanging out with random 15/16 year olds
While, at the same time, having the same level of social maturity as them, in practice.
She felt like a total child
As well as a complete hand-out
Who does not do any work to save her life.

As she heats up what effectively amounts to a symbol of the time when she was eating rations every day

The same ingredients,
The same flavour and the same square-shaped vacuum-sealed container
Every. Single. Day.

But surely
These are so tasty. It will make her feel better. otherwise,
Other people would insist on always asking: "Did you eat well?"
So that is what she did.
She eats her prescribed nutrient intake in less than 5 minutes...
Which felt oddly grounding…
And then went to bed. Still dressed with the same Kispo overall
And the same
Unwashed flannel shirt that she has been wearing for a week.
