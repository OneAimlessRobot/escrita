```
Grinding bones

my limbs jitter with anxiety,
my pathetic coping mechanisms are not an option, anymore.
hollow like plumbing,
what the world gives me
goes straight down.

The stomach for beauty is both tired
and lazy like never before
churning on dated memories.

The days feel so similar that they almost feel symmetric,
and time passes
with me barely changing.


bugs still scare me,
as do heights
and water.

And worst of all...
is rejection.

I cant fathom being unwanted just for this...
I was turned.
not persuaded!

I wont.
Its true that no one really cares,
yes...

But i need to learn to love again.
i need to find out if I still can.

Drawing with blood,
carving shapes from trees...
i take from the environment just to make my silly art.

The judge says Im guilty,
but my heart says: "Just five more minutes."

And yet,
there is this cloud over my head...
the lack of control that I patch over with jokes,
the bits,
the rose tinted memories of my stupid years
that turn out to be complete stupidity...

Maybe my existence really is just a narrativized walk of shame.

A circus I was conscripted to
a theatre with ethics and steel.



It happened 12 years ago
it still feels unbelievable.


```
