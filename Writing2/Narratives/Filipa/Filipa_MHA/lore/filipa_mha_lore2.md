More adjustments to "Gamebreaker":
Even if she does not have a full Gamebreaker complete,
She experiences a mild form of Gamebreaker while she is in combo:
Upon completing successive feints
or air juggling tricks without getting tackled or failing,
The ball takes on a scarlet red glow,
Displaying a trail that extends
a meter beyond its last travelled direction
After being put into motion for more than 10 seconds
And not being still for more than 10 seconds.

Her eyes will also glow a bright,
Digital orange through her hollow retinas
And a purple-hued, bright bolt of lightning
will irradiate from her forehead.

The glows will all get noticeably more bright
with every incrementing combo unit

This is important to know because opponents
can antecipate a gamebreaker this way.

Anyways:
The higher the combo multiplier,
Which increments by one for every feint performed,
The stronger the powershot will be.
And,
For any target Filipa had in mind before performing a powershot,
The more score charge a successful hit will give her.
Filipa can,
If there is a reason for it,
End a combo on her terms in two ways:
By taking aforementioned powershot,
Simply waiting a full second
without performing skills with the metal ball,
(The final combo score gets tallied in the case of these two,
With an extra boost given in case of a powershot)
Or by failing an air trick
(The combo is broken in this case and score isnt tallied)


Leaving a combo by any means does [not] erase any gamebreaker score charge.
But combo points only get tallied at the end
If it ends "properly".

More details and nuances:
Performing a "round the world" air juggle and stopping
does not count as a combo.
Performing two of those successfully [does],
But the returns diminish if skills get repeated.
But the rule is simple:
A single type of air juggle
performed twice in a row diminishes returns.
But alternating two different ones is enough to bypass this filter.
The more effort she felt while beating someone,
The higher the score she gets and,
Similar to air juggle tricks,
beating the exact same opponent twice will diminish returns
And the returns diminish by reducing the added score by half.

This added score is a number that,
To get the total ammount of points given by a combo,
Is then further multiplied by the N of skills/beats performed.

By the way:
The physical/mental/hormonal/quirk enhancements
experienced by Filipa during even something like a 5x combo
are a substantially less
than that of being in an actual gamebreaker mode.

If externally interrupted during a valid one,
She will experience some noteworthy physical drawbacks
albeit not nearly at the same level as on a Gamebreaker,
But, for a couple of seconds,

("A couple of seconds" depends on how high
(How "good" it was feeling)
the combo was.
Potentially ranging from 3 seconds,
To almost half a minute.)

The eardrums will ring in a way that is
from mildly unpleasent to "it compromises her situational awareness",
Filipa will feel a disorientation
akin to getting out of bed in the morning suddenly
while having poor blood circulation
And will get rather frustrated at herself,
Becoming locked into a scripted animation of cursing
and sulking at herself before moving on.
