Speedrotting emotional death

```


I dont wanna get a company job
I am autistic and highly specialized.
I failed the cognitive tests in 9th grade.

Im just an obsessed hobbyist...
Im not creative at all
Everyone else got results in all areas.
I just got results in arithmetic.
Everything I can do
They can do better.

But...
Why do my friends praised my writing so much?

Why do I feel like a fraud
No matter what I do
No matter what I survive
I am always chasing standards
And can never settle with what is already achieved.

In practice,
This generates thrust.
But spiritually...
I am rotting.
Every 
Single
Day.

Every day thinking they are too busy for me to call
So many days alone,
chasing ghosts of the past.
So many sunny days spent isolated at home
Talking with dolls inside my head.

When will it end, I wonder...
When will I ever feel
Okay?


```
