Dehydrated.  
  
Imagine being in the ocean,  
and feeling your eyes crystalize,  
dry with longing for recognition.  
As the saying goes:  
The man is only sad about what he doesnt have.  
  
so...  
I spend time in the ocean.  
The prosperity,  
the enigmatic depth from the big blue seas,  
all the variety and possibilities?  
they are all alien to me.  
I hear seagulls,  
Fish swim around my lonesome,  
meandering boat  
and waves gently rock me into a calm trance as I look at the sun.  
  
Its hot, now.  
its stormy tomorrow.  
its quiet.  
always.  
It is happy?  
never.  
Is it content?  
...  
eventually.  
  
There is an ocean of affection around me that I cant touch.  
Sinking my nets to its bottom,  
I always hope for the best.  
But the fibers never strain.  
Yet they get brittle  
they break.  
  
I become porous  
the mesh gets bigger.  
But what goes through will eventually leave  
so I stay hungry,  
I stay thirst.  
  
I stay dehydrated  
  
...  
  
"Dehydrated" by Lena de Verías, 2026
