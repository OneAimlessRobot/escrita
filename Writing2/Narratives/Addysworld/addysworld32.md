Slowburn.

Threads of attachment around my neck
sap away the oxygen of freedom.
with only eyes for you,
Life without feels empty.

Gently caressing my soul
Until I see you again,
I try not to look in the mirror
or else I would just see half of a face.

Cold shivers at night
while I wait for school to start in the morning.
I know you will be there.
You always will.
Right?

So I do everything I can to make it last.
I imagine myself with you to cope with absense.

and Lose myself in long rants where I just end up bitter.
And angry.
And embarassed.

I wear these clothes

You complete me.
Dont judge me.
You fascinate and...
Make me feel alive.

When the addiction stops?
whats left?
I hold onto this fantasy
for all the sugar it can give me

"I should have been better."
You and your fancy "other friends."

my face pales.
"Its now that you will disappear.
isnt it?"
