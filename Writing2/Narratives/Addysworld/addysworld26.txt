addysworld26

I want to talk about bullying.


when she already has scars in her heart
all with my name on it,
does it even matter
how much I change around her anymore?
Ill answer: It doesnt matter.
It doesnt.
The city is now rubble.
And destroyed cities cannot be rebuilt with old blueprints.
They will stay frozen.
It will stay in tatters
until the governors invent new ways to build.

None of us have the power to fix others.
Only to watch
And learn from our dirty mistakes.
Our awkward phases.
Our misaligned minds.
And our cruel fantasies of control.
Understanding is not a patch.
Its a blood sample from the wound we made when we were too petty
to admit that they were needed.
Not necessarily by others,
and mostly just by us,
the aggressors.

We are walking rooms of mirrors,
Deflecting blame as if we were free from the guilt of handling the souls of others like hot potatoes
passing them around like torches of abuse
Contemplating our handy work
To avoid having to stop,
And admit
That we were the wrong ones
All along.
All along.
