addysworld8




Im so...
confused.

My thoughts...
wont start.

ive spent so much time in this damned streamer
and got it to play over the network and--
...

I forgot why life even matters, anymore.

Im just absorbing everything that enters my system, right now:
1- Online game beef
2- Politics
3- Gender biased statements from now defunct politicians,
which also claimed that "empathy" was dangerous.

I have just...
the [worst].
[worst] cold.

No shot.
Just syrup.
and a body that is routinely underfed.

There was even one day where I woke up worse than yesterday

my emotions.
are flat.

my eyes see things.
and that's all they see.

the screen in my phone?
its cracked.

the skin in my fingers?
its ripped.

there is a blood stain
on the wrist of my sleeve.

its cold.
I pace more.
I feel more jittery



but that is not the point!

I feel more alone with every passing day.
and...
with every passing month
I feel like a new armaggedon had just gone by.
I somehow keep finding more ways of feeling more,
and more empty
that...
always surprise me!


I made this streamer.
it streams in mp3, now.
cool upgrade.

I make the display queue sizes customizable
but still have no idea where my older brother is.
what happened to him.
...
and whether or not Im a bastard for barely calling him.

people assume things.
but we dont dislike eachother.
I may disappoint him.
all the time.
for being useless and slow.
Dumb and naiive,
but he kept helping me when he could.

and now he is gone.
I have no idea where he is.

and I cant even apologize.
I cant shower him in apology,
swearing on my life
to be less inconvenient next time.

That is what I always do.
I dont know how to explain anything that happens to me.
I think its intellect.
But its actually just passivity,
and a refusal to let people approach me in any way.


nothing changes or dissolves.
everything just...
precipitates.

hunks of gooey solid
floating on greyed out,
worn oil.

that is what I feel.

this damned streamer.
it took everything to get it to work.
it took it all...
from me.

The coughing does not stop,
catharsis halting.
thoughts breaking
windows of light shattering into opacity.

I cough once per every sentence.
And try to make a song.
coughing fits that destroy
all the love I put into...

Im sorry, brother.
I know.
I know.
College is easier.
Work is hard.
Im irresponsible.
Im wasting potential, I know.
You know what is best for me.
But I can only live
in a world that makes me feel alive,
and I am not allowed to, so...
I crash.


I stopped trying to scream
because coughing would do it for me, before.
Now all I do is joke and quip
to a god that has no smile.

thats it for today.

Im empty.

Goodbye.
