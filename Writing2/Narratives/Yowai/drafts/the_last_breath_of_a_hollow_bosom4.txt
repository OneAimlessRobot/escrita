Title: "Yowai is weak/Disorder"

Im not having lunch, right now.
Im laying in bed and sick out of my mind.
My body feels horrible
and sneezing gives me nosebleeds every other time.
Focused on a specific corner of the wall
where a spider has built a web,
I listen as the house clock
on the hallway ticks by mechanical, precise
and tempered
but relentless. My ears ring in disorientation every time I shuffle in my sheets
alone in this house to take care of myself.
My legs hurt when they rub together. My privates are sore from the orange urine that secretes into a diaper that I have to replace every meal. No matter how many calories I eat, I always feel tired.
Tired. Tired. Tired.
Im counting the sudden cramps I get in my left knee
As the rain pours gently into the window
Sideways and thin from a midly overcast sky
White and... oddly warm
in yet another saturday afternoon
in november. My nose is crammed with choking congestion from the bridge down
But the smell of smoke and blood comes out 
Every time I cough. The pathogens are doing their work very well. They keep me bedridden
without working
with nausea holding me hostage for yet another spurt of half digested lunch. I tell it: "Please. I have no lunch. Leave me alone." But it does not care.
I lean towards a bucket I have to my left
where my bedside table is
As peristaltic convulsions urge my body: "Give it. Give the lunch back to the outside. We do not want it here."
I should have resisted... Because there is not even anything that can come out. Only drool faintly colored like pastel makeup
that smells like a bitter sweet substance. 
The body forces everything into it. Eventually,
my throat becomes so sore from agonising grunting, that blood,
mixed with vibrantly colored, runny mucus comes out. Tears come out of my eyes from the pain.
This is my life as the sick maid. Every time I catch just the slightest cold,
I get bedridden. What a lame quirk I had to be born with. Im only 28. But in my mind, It is as if I have already retired.
What am I doing this saturday? Nothing important.
Im just waiting patiently
Until autoimmune diseases eventually wipe us all off the face of the earth.
