```
Mrs. Cressida (U. A. Hallway Sweeper):



Cressida is a 37 year old woman
who was born with a degenerative quirk which I decided to call:
"Hyper-reactive Immune system disorder".
It causes her immune system to become disproportionate
And metabolically wasteful
Exaggerating any mild pathogen-related illness that she develops
or even something simple like inhaling sawdust
Potentially bed ridding her.
and leaves her permanently exhausted
requiring Yowai to live an efficient and pragmatic life.
She is content with herself
and is grateful with the little things.
The quirk offers no other advantages.

She is quite fatalistic and dry
but surprisingly assertive for how reserved she is.
Yowai likes chirping out unsolicited health advice to random students
whenever the hallways are quieter
and has a very simple worldview:

the purpose of being born
is to work.
Why do we need to work?
because we need money.
The money is used to buy glucose/calories
which then get turned into energy
to do more work
and earn money
so on and so forth.

During her childhood, Cressida love(s)d nature documentaries, specially those that portray detailed descriptions of the biology and inner workings of animals
with emphasis on their digestive systems and metabolism
and used to fantasize about being a hibernating animal
while hiding in a pillow fort
all with National Geographic being broadcasted on her bedroom TV.
Whenever she gets a serious flu...
or some worse pathogen, Cressida may even end up in the hospital.
Luckly, she is not a picky eater
and has no food allergies. Her breakfast, for example, is a very warm bowl of oat meal, minced meat, broccoli AND fruit salad
that she eats in every single one of her meals [1]. She appreciates art and Music
but does not go out of her way to interact with those things.
preferring to keep earning her money
and buying her glucose. Her favourite time of the day is early morning
as she wakes up quite early to take care of the school
and also have time for a little nap just before the students arrive. Her pronouns are she/her, her height is 170cm tall and  her weight is 58 kg,
resulting in a build that is thin
but not unhealthy. She has a bachelor in Microbiology as she was always curious about the subject
and enjoys listening to shoegaze music whenever Tokoyami plays it at the boys lockerroom.
She has very little patience for drama
and always prefers to withdraw over getting aggravated.
Something that needs mentioning
is that medicine has not yet adapted to the existence of degenerative quirks
and the medicine they sell to mitigate the symptoms of her constant illnesses is too expensive for her minimum wage job to afford.
(Issues like these are one of the justifications that Villains use to form criminal groups)
She once submitted a resume to a chemistry lab for a job
but was rejected
so she gave up
and now sweeps hallways.
It does not really bother her that much. During the winter, due to how often Cressida Blows her nose, it is not uncommon for her to get severe nosebleeds.

Her demeanour is lazy and listless bordering on disinterested.
However,
despite approaching things with a detached mindset
she is very caring towards the students of the hallways that she maintains.


Her favourite fruit is the banana
as it is easy to peel and easy to chew.

Mrs. Cressida owns a 1997 Honda Today
Which she drives to school every day.

Mrs. Cressida has long, black hair, black eyes, a small nose and a resting sad-looking expression.
(Even if she is not sad as often as people think)

Mrs. Cressida is conscientious
and always managed to get solid grades
despite skipping most of middle school due to constant illness related absence,
always enjoying doing the homework that got delivered to her home,
never truly feeling alone due to support from classmates.


```


Her fitness is very bad.
she runs very slow,
needs reassurance before attempting a simple two footed jump
and does not like exerting her body unnecessarily, in general.

And one of the side effects of her medication
Is that it makes her blood thinner

Cressida gets her medicine illegally from Chisaki as they are essentially quirk suppressants that dull it down.
She directly contributes to his research
and even pays him for the bottles.


There was one day where she woke up
dizzy and weakened
with a pillow drenched in her blood.

She has an IQ of 105

and this is the part where it ends
because I have nothing else to say about her, for now.

Mrs. Cressida

One of the Hallway sweepers of all time
