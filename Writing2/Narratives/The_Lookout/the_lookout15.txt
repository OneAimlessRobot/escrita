The chasm of stasis:
An introspective text by ksun Ayanami


"
Am I dreaming?
...
Am I
...
...
Are people
Going to understand?
Do I understand what I understand?
Or did chatbots delude me?
...
But I see it.
...
People's minds
Cluster around...
Tendencies
Groups of.
And...
That creates incompatibilities
Between people.
This text is about MBTI.
Again.
And its 16 groups of people.
These groups
In my mind,
Are generalized...
Estimations created by Carl Jung
With the purpose,
Initially,
To systematize
And categorize
Different families of attitudes,
Differnet cognitive playstyles
Of philosophers,
Thinkers,
Or even people at large.
...
You may think
that being like another person
Person A
will require the same amount of sacrifice
The same amount of pain as another.
But youre wrong.
...
There will always be
A group of people
Of which the perspectives are valid
Or even similar...
But.
...
The way to process,
The way of forming interpretations
And value or action based judgements
Will be different.
Sometimes in ways that,
When heard once,
Feel wrong.
And that when imposed on a person
And too distinct
from how they naturally navigate the world
Feel...
Suffocating
Almost erasing.
I cannot explain it,
But some people
Just feel like other people
In reverse.
...
These families,
These categories of people
Operate on modes.
And these modes are linkable
By tolerance of similar-enough personalities.
...
How closely similar two people of different groups have to be
In order for connection to have
Tolerably low amounts of friction, well...
I feel like that depends
On how aware of these groups the involved parties are.
...
in MBTI they have a name,
But these names are used everywhere.
...
These are always framed with different imagery
Sometimes more abstractly
Other times less,
...
But there just [are] groups of people.
Like--
Come on.
...
Are you telling me
That someone that spends their life
concerned about logistics,
And efficiency
And maintenance
Can possibly reach conclusions
In the same way of someone
Perpetually concerned with originality
Self expression
And identity vividness?
...
And I know.
I am explaining the model
Using the model itself.
...
But if you lived enough of a life
You [will] know what I am talking about.
...
People either care
or not care about
the broad life perspectives of others
And bringing them into agreeing on everything
Is not productive on the long, long term.
In a world with perfect harmony,
Everyone lives according to ultimate suitability.
...
That is my take.
Logistics people
Will always wobble, and wobble, and wobble...
but if you zoom the microscope out.
...
they occupy the same region of space.
and their center,
in this particular example,
Will always be rigor,
Maintenance
And engineering the world around them
To be as smooth,
And understandable as possible for others..
These people make good managers,
Engineers
And lawyers.
...
Youre not going to give that job
To someone constantly concerned
About advocacy, right?
...
It just does not make sense to me.
It doesnt.
It is [not] broad.
it is [correct].
