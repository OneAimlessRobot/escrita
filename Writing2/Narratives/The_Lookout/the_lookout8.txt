...
Frustration
is the hardest emotion to process.
Not love.
Because when youre in love,
You can dream,
You get energy
And the world seems more colorful.
When youre sad,
Time seems to run slower,
The thoughts are easier to read...
In yourselfs and others.
The tears wash away tension
that we tend to hold back.
When we are frustrated,
We also tend to get angry.
Because its as if the world is mocking us
And that it had been
Since we can remember.
Grief has legal priority,
Love has social priority,
But frustration...
It is envy,
It is anger,
It is ugly
And the actions it entices one to do
Are inexcusable.
Because we lack ability,
Composure,
Motivation,
positive thinking. We do not get taught to get over it
Because we plan our lives around avoiding it.
We pick a life for our talents,
Not for ourselves.
We avoid competition
Because not being great at the beginning
Is almost as bad as a life's worth of failure.
We may feel frustrated
When unable to matter,
When too dull to be noticed,
Or unfairly judged. Those are situations
That only bring out the worst of us:
We tilt,
We flail
We scream
And even...
Cry.
There is no table strong enough for the hit,
There is no street wide enough for the scream
And there is no honour
In whinning like losers.
Whether we complain,
Reframe
Or simply ignore,
The truth...
is that if we feel frustrated,
No one
Is coming to help us.
