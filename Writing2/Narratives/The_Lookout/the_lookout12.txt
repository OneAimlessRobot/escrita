"Milk Outside" visual novel review
...
Have you played Milk Inside
Or milk Outside?
As far as I can tell...
They are about a traumatized girl
Who became increasingly more isolated as she grew up
And her neurodivergence
Obsessive routines
And avoidant behaviour
Lead her to developing  harsh inner voice
That we control
In order for her to complete tasks within her world
Like buying milk
Or rummaging her room for her to "gather thoughts" before bed.
The thoughts are compared to fireflies.
Which hide behind 
Or inside items
That she barely touches.
She confused being in a mental institution
With being a student,
Interprets seeing bizarre renderings of herself in the mirror
As totally normal
And sometimes hallucinates in real life
Like imagining that she is falling from height
While in the balcony
...
Comforting...?
No.
We are mostly there to be direct
And make sure she takes pills
Shaming her, otherwise
...
Mismanaging...?
id argue that no.
We do not mismanage her.
But...
She is very clumsy,
To euphemise it.
For example:
There is a pill bottle on her sink
At the start of the game
And it is so old
And out of date
That it melded into a single liquid.
She drinks the entire liquid in one go
Before we clean her room.
The thoughts go away
And are slower,
Easier to control.
But an incredible pain
That coats her entire body
Comes a few minutes after
...
I respect how unapolagetically
The game asserts
That mental illness
Is not some quirky gimmick, in practice.
It is not just "Bedrotting"
or being "lazy"...
Those are the exact stigma
That mentally ill people
[Loathe] to hear.
