"Hello,
Im ksun Ayanami.
I came to talk to you
About cosmology.
...
I watched a video
From a hobbyist researcher
Telling me about articles he looked up
Which showed that life on earth
Is unlikely.
...
It made complete sense.
And with all of the climate things
That we have been experiencing
Over the last few decades,
Ive came to the conclusion
That people have been stretching their luck
For ages.
...
We extract,
We pollute, exploit and abuse
The earth,
Other forms of life,
And the constant wars,
The conflict,
The petty skirmishes...
Are such a waste of time.
We just waste time.
For influence over each other,
For superiority,
To assert ourselves
In a world that,
In practice,
Does not care, does not discriminate,
...
And that we may be forced to share
For as long as we exist as a species.
...
Since ive moved to this house,
I noticed something:
Nothing in me changed. I feel the same things,
I have the same fears,
Say the same things
And even dress the same way.
Despite that,
I still squabble,
involving myself in meaningless rivalries,
I bought a car,
A bed,
...
And,
As a trade,
the earth grows more bare, poorer
And less satisifed, elsewhere.
The things we have,
Are not measurements of our effort
But of how much is available.
The world is one big ball of recycled money
That we funnel into meaningless toys
That become scrap.
We syphon the life out of nature
To power our electronics,
To power our mining drills,
Which give us the minerals
To produce devices like the one I write on
...
But as a species...
We are depleting ourselves.
Gassing out.
It is estimated that our obsession
With artificial intelligence
Besides enabling a society
Increasingly stripped of ownership
Over its own tools for survival:
Intelligence, creativity...
Even physical power
since the advent of the otto engine,
The steam machine...
Which were all useful, in retrospective.
...
But technology is become a goal
And not a tool.
We innovate
Only to innovate faster.
We speed up our efforts
But the effort leads to nothing that improves happiness,
satisfaction
...
Or a fundamentally better way to live life.
Religion has given us all of the answers
that it has got,
Science is merely done for fun, at this point
Sports fans become more and more disillusioned
And the children grow dissociated from the pain
Only to get confused once it hits them, too.
...
We are mercenaries for comfort
Fighting every day to make our lives easier.
We work,
We work,
We work.
...
But in the end,
Deep down we know that it wont matter.
Everyone feels it.
The end is near.
...
The rich, corrupt leaders
prepare themselves for the worst
Us, the small ones
Pray that the final blow
will not hurt too badly.
Acceleration towards rotting
That is what I predict.
Ive opened myself to be proved wrong for so long...
But that moment will never come.
I am alone,
You are alone
And humans do not give a single damn
About each other."





Goodbye~
