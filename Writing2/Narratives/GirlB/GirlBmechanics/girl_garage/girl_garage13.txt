Streaming music is pretty lightweight.

And the reason that it is lightweight
is because
Specially with compressed formats like mp3
for which the developed methods of decompression into raw pcm
Are varied
and very effective
as well as cpu light
streaming is like doing very simple IO
Both at client and server side
...
At networking speeds.
And networking is (not necessarily
but conceptually)
always slower than the average long term storage device
And specially much slower than accessing volatile memory
Like RAM
Which is where 90% of accesses and writings happen, anyway.
And most of the time is spent writing to the audio device
which has its own timing
and can be done sequentially
instead of concurrently.
It takes its time to finish
And the program has plenty of time to rest, meanwhile.

So
That is my take on why streaming music is,
Fundamentally,
A lightweight endeavour for a computer.
