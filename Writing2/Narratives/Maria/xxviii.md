xviii

Metallic emission
By Maria


```
"There was something...
so sweet about not knowing a thing.
But,
like tough clay that begins as soft,
fertile soil,
the mind hardens to match the world around it.
It molds,
it changes
it learns...
...
and it cracks.

So many times.
And so,
rolling with the violent shaking of a mind that refuses to comprehend its own misery,
I linger on the walls of this castle
Always ready to sell myself to the world like specialized equipment.

My youth has faded.
I threw it away because adulthood crushed it out of me.

I still miss it,
but stopped caring a long,
long time ago.

The tears stopped coming out
now they just...
seep out of me like osmosis.

The salt on my skin brightens the shiny, clear shell I wear.
Rock, paint and iron make up my organism
And the waxed marble that fills up its walls does not insulate.
it contains,
it protects and it separates.
But there are no windows.
there is a fridge
a stove
a vase and a bed.
The door opens... and i am suddenly out.

My life is bare like the skin of a baby
but without any of their potential.
I am thinned out,
dried to a crisp by erosion.

One day...
I met a friend who taught me how to swim.

They looked...
so concerned when they saw me in swimming attire.
There was not an ounce of bone in my body that they couldnt see.

the heart beat through the skin it touched,
walking felt like a chore...
and the fat that was supposed to be in my body,
made it hard to stay afloat,
to swim like they wanted.
so I tried harder,
and as I did...
I only sank farther, and so...
they eventually gave up on me

a mechanical raven...
once lent me its wings.

Like gravity,
I avoid the pain as it above physics.
Cleaning and duties are my salvation
as I run from the purge
from the introspective tendency
urging me to wake up
like the plague.
I mourn the death of a self which had the energy.
the vibrancy to withstand being so busy.
But now...
everything tires me out.
The voice that detaches
that watches from above sees a trail of soul clinging to the soles of my feet
barely hanging on.

Around me,
is an air filled with the sighs of each day of work
each day filled with the urge for something that gives me no choice...
but to stop
and be alone with myself.

The carriage has one wheel on the road
and another in the mud.
And when it eventually tips over...
I will pay for it.

As the albatross of reason watches on
ready to address me as I stumble about in the white hot desert,
the stale,
warm air that fills my lungs
with the ascetic pride of being a living utensil gradually makes my blood acidic,
burning those who ever touch it.
Its cells carry what the brain wont think about:
The loss of time,
of life,
of youth...
It carries the tension one refuses to release,
the complaining that could get me out...
but it is no good when others have to deal with it.
Deal with [me].
I am a terror for existing
and those who witness me before them should be afraid.

Well,
not for themselves
but for me.
As must let myself scream,
for it is too late, now.
The fuse will burn all the way, one day.

But I hope the aftermath will at least leave fireworks behind.

Something that at least reminds everyone that...
in these deserts,
there was once life."
```
