Title: "Maria Henriques (27yr support hero)"

Maria Henriques

Gender: female

Age: 27

Sexuality: Hetero


Appearance: Tall
Thin
Worn-out,
Frayed,
Hay-colored hair
subtle tiredness in grey eyes
Awkward movement
She wears a NASA sweater,
Brown twill pants
And several friendship bracelets in her left wrist.

Personality: Maria is reserved, responsible
And has pronounced apathy towards media/entertainment.
She is uptight,
Moralizing
And injects world politics into everything.
Trying to have a normal conversation with her
Is an extremely unpleasant
And often hopeless endeavour
Maria is very loyal to duty
But will become very vocal
(Often to the point of shouting)
If witnessing someone wasting food/Disobeying rules.
She is punctual and rigorous
But inflexible to the point of lacking empathy
Attends charity events and pride parades.
Public displays of affection gross her out.
Her favorite food is a Portuguese dish called "Bacalhau à Gomes de Sá".
She is judgemental, absolutist
And reluctant to accept new approaches to problems
She enjoys doing volunteer work wherever she can
Whether it is aiding at construction sites,
Providing brunt during rescue operations
(such as carrying heavy loads/supplies
To deliver to natural disaster victims)
But does not like fighting.
At all.
Whenever she is relaxed,
Her speech delivery is measured
And the tone of her voice is soft.
But
When she gets tilted,
Her engines (see quirk)
Will fire on all cylinders
And the shouts that she produces
Will make your chest vibrate so much
That you will feel like you are at an airport
And your ears will (Not actually)
blow up.
Maria is exhausting to be around
performs solidarity
And her sense of moral debt
(See backstory)
Is so extreme
That she has went to church one time
And prayed for a fire to happen
Just so that she could go put it out
Alongside the volunteer firefighters.



Backstory: (trigger warning: Severe illness)
Maria is an ex oncological patient
Who was raised in relative scarcity.
Her family members would allocate left overs to her
And it always made her feel guilty.
When she got better,
After several donations from locals in her hometown
As well as support during chemo,
Maria has developed this vague,
Unquenchable sense of moral debt towards everyone
And experiences an eternal urge
To work as much as possible
While receiving as little as possible.


Quirk:
Diesel engine
five small diesel motors
One in each limb
And one in her core
Provide loads of torque at low operational speed.
What this means is that she cannot hurl objects very fast
But she can perform very heavy,
But slow
Sustained lifts.
These engines are very loud
And the fuel comes partly from the food she eats.
Each engine in each limb
Has an exhaust that reveals itself when the respective joints bend
And the exhaust pipe for her core engine
Is on her behind.
This last engine is the most important
As it reduces the effort required
By all the other engines.
Sustained high efforts will give Maria crappy headaches
And the constant noise leaves her very irritable
Do not talk to her after work
Or do anything "fun" near her, either.
She is like that one uptight student
Except that she actually says what you think she is gonna say.

In college,
Where she took a degree in mechanical engineering,
her nickname was "Diesel H***r"
Due to how absolute
And uptight she was.
