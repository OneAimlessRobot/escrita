The mighty 300 kW boxer 6 washes down the mulsane straight
And Jessica gets ready to estimate the braking zone.
These comfort tires make the ranges rather unusual to judge
And braking has to be pondered to save them.
As a buffetin rattles on the windshield
And tiny grains of asphalt zip past at extraordinary speed while blending into a grey blurr,
The car has exceeded two hundred and NINETY kilometers per hour
which is close to the RUF machine's aerodynamic top speed!
But way before the chicane, Jessica starts to brake.
...
She feathers the throttle at the beginning.
But being hard to stay at low input without ABS, her foot suddenly sinks!
Tires scrub loudly against the tarmac, stuck in their wheelhubs
as Jessica's braking distance doubles.
She ALMOST avoids the tire walls on corner entry
But has to slow down even more to go through the escape path.
...
After meandering through the barriers to get back on the circuit
With way less exit speed that would be required to have a successful first lap in this challenge,
Jessica  fists the steering wheel in frustration
Briefly honking the horn. It was still a street car, after all.

"MERDA!"
She yells, making her molars hurt from how tightly she clenches her jaw.
She MASHES the throttle, once more
Putting the budget rubber through an absolute ringer
As 4 of the tires lift up plumes of smoke
Driven by the all wheel drivetrain layout.

...

After a short while,
Jessica is back on top speed! 
And the final third of the mulsane is now over.
Jessica starts braking now, suspecting it will be a good range.

"Travas agora, não travas?
Foda-se... É preciso marrar essa porra a 30 quilómetros..."

Jessica's frustration took less than an entire lap to set in.
The porsche team running this drill is paying attention.
What will they have to say before she comes in to the pits?
She starts braking right after the car goes airborn for a moment
As there is a hill in the middle of the braking zone for this cumbersome setup.
[truuuuuuuuummmmmm....
...
...
TRUMmmmmmmm...

]
Jessica goes down the gears at snails pace
Testing her on nerves in NOT locking the wheels this time.
But it just takes FOREVER.

She ends up with good speed for corner entry on this hairpin
But it ends up being very unoptimized
As the car did not struggle to rotate as much as Jessica thinks it should.

"Tinha mais aqui...
Prontos, lá se vai esta,
P$rra..."

She accelerates down another straight which is not as long
And is where the famous clk mercedes took off and flipped in that one race.
It features two very troublesome corners:
One very high-speed right hander, which a typical GT car or Le-Mans prototype typically does not need to think about
And another one.
Jessica actually needed to blip the brakes briefly
Bringing more of the weight to the front and help the car turn.
She was going 150 kilometers per hour
And had to half-gas through the entire thing.

Jessica is not having a good time. Her instincts fight with the car
And when Jessica cannot do what she wants,
She gets mad.

"Tod'á puta da pista é pa travar!
Porra..."
She says, complaining.
Now this next corner is crucial and requires braking just a few ten meters out of the previous turn.
Jessica starts braking somewhere in the midpoint between the two bends
But it turns out to be [way] too late! She cannot control herself
And mashes the brakes. Mashes them HARD--
And they lock!
The car goes straight as an arrow into the gravel trap
And regardless of how hard Jessica turns,
She was not able to avoid two wheels on the coarse pebbels.
Her racing line is ruined for the next turn
That she enters at with way too little speed. The rythm is all over the place...
And just before the next turn,
A 90 degree right hander, we get to witness another honk from the horn.
She brakes carefully, this time as the speed is lower
...
Just before the turn, it was as if she was parking the car.
The engine lulls for a while
before Jessica gives it a smooth steering angle
Which she is forced to do to not lock up the wheels.
It frustrates her aggressive V line nature
That she first adopted when racing in the karts
And the way the less rigid street going chassis gently rolls to the outside of the bend aggravates the Portuguese driver on a visceral level.
The right hander is taken at just shy of 20 kilometers per hour.
As the lap continues.
