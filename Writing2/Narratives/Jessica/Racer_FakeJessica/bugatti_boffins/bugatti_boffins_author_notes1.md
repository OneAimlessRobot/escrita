[author notes:

"
This one...
Like all my others,
I just wrote it all as it came in my mind...
and steering...
correcting half way through
rewriting would make it spin out.
thats what I felt

And so,
because of that,
it still feels like I am an amateur.
like I dont know what Im doing.
like my sincerity is just meant to be flattened into something I didnt achieve on my own.
you dont validate my text.
you correct it.

It proves the voices that they are dead right.
I dont create my art.
I just write it out.
I was born to steal.
I learn.
I dont create.
I mesmerize myself at what others do.
Feel envy.
The way they publish.
the way they survive on joy and chasing their interiority.
When im corrected,
I feel like nothing more than some farmer in a tent
where everyone else is a bonefide boffin of the game.
It erases me.
Because if something isnt mine in what comes out of my hands,
then everything is a copy.
I just wish I could find myself on my own.
Without others looking in.
For my spirit to resist erasure just long enough for the stem to grow the poison that keeps parasitic thoughts away.
But I was born rigid.
I cannot handle true competence
Because my mind is chasing pleasure.
Beauty. Organic like a blooming flower,
Biased. Preferential, Specific and sensorial.
Im a plant that sucks what it becomes from the soil. I wont flow like others who rip the minerals from the rock they are poured into.

I feel like time speeds up when others look at my writing.

As if I just set them on fire.
Urgently waiting for me to save them.

Being told to improve makes me feel like my arms are getting dragged down by dogs that dont exist.
My entrails feel lightweight,
as they float within their container.
The mind formats.
As I cannot decide whether I float down to the earth,
Or sink upward
into isolated space

That is why I should keep my mouth shut.

Or my babies.
they will suffer.
"]
