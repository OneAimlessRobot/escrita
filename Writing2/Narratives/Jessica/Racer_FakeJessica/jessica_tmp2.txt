Slowburn

Chasing every spec of dust down
in a cluttered field of abandoned trees
the wind that washes by my neck fills me with shivers.


The sun that used to warm me
feels sharp,
Irritating.

The friends that talk to me
feel like nuisances

And the radio I tuned into to relax only makes me ache for the moment it shuts the hell up.

Sleep has been replaced with the warmth
of baking my own insides for nourishment

All joy has to be built
and none of it is to be kept.

The sewage of embarassing memories comes washing by when yet another fly comes buzzing in my ear.

I swat it away

"Damn you!
Get lost!"
...
but it just turned out to be something telling me to go eat something.

It will probably not warn me again
from how unfairly I addressed it, the poor thing...

And then,
There is nowhere comfortable.

The shade feels like an annoying windy day.
It teases me with its chilly air.
the sun blinds me.

Whenever I forget about even the slightest of errands,
The burning compels me to scream,
Grunt and whine in public
Cynnicism is the philosophy,
frustration is the outcome
and jitters...
even under warm blankets at night,
wont go away.

Im burning so slowly...

And I cannot hope enough
for when I am finally
Out of gas.

No fake "I must rest soon, or else" fluff.

But for a time where exaustion stops being a nuiscance
And enforces its will, instead.

I am tired.

I should go to bed, now.

Slowburn.
