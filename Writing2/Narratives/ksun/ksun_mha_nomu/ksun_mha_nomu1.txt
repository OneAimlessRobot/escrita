With ksun being one of the first to be freed from its enclosure.

and could no longer reproduce.

She had been experimed on as a cruel joke.
To be given a quirk that turns you into the monster you are afraid you are,
All for One did this
spefically due to an interaction they had on one fateful day.
ksun was gaming in her room when suddenly--
having snuck past all security,
He makes it to her doorstep
and knocks.

[knock, knock]
ksun,
always the unpolite,
unashamed and unamused gamer,
flips them a metaphorical,
verbal birdie
and keeps playing
as ksun has no idea of who they are.

They turned out to be:

All
for
One.

They began teasing,
playing mindgames with each other
until ksun,
who was quirkless at the time,
decides that she was playing with death.
so she attempts to contact Aizawa
but getting no answer.
Resourceful,
she gathers her blankets,
tying them together to make a rope
performing a quick test on it
by tugging 
with it wrapped to one of her bed posts.

she goes outside,
wraps the rope to a beam on the balcony, and then,
carefully,
trying to remain quiet,
she ends up escaping all for one by climbing down to the patio!
It was only a mere four meters
but she was quirkless
and not exactly athletic, either.

She immediatly heads over to the receptionist
complaining,
justifiably so,
in pure outrage
at how All for One was "In her god damned room a second ago"
and that "nobody had done anything".

Later on,
Aizawa replies to the calls he has been getting
pulling up to the site shortly after.

as advised by ksun

getting him no option but to book it, once more
as he figured that this was not the time for an all out conflict yet.


ksun had outsmarted All for One.
who underestimated her.
and so?
In a later mission where she was deep into a fortified villain base,
acting as a spy
she got caught
having her communication equipment confiscated and overriden.
with a voice mimicking quirk,
All for one Sent back false declarations of betrayal in her place,
right in front of her limped corpse,
drugged and unconscious.

Later on,
although maintaining her irreverant and sassy personality
she gets turned into a weapon.
She ejects spores out of her back
that she controls with her fingers.
these latch onto enemies and override their organisms,
acting as a new organ
that feeds ksun via a neurolink protocol.
these can feed her for as long as the host has the spore latched onto their body
which is extremely painful to remove
as well as requiring invasive surgery to detach.

She has always seen herself as a parasite.
never quite strong enough to justify outsmarting her problems,
she was always seen as an "undying,
cowardly witch"
that played mind games,
skipped out on social events and logistical responsibilities
and was generally abrasive and unpleasent to be around.

so?
as a cruel joke,
she gets turned into a spore machine.
to feed on the strength of others when she innevitably craves it again,
and again.
with the quirk becoming more addictive to use every time.

healing up.

until--

still unconscious,
Dabi had been ordered to bring her to a specific location on the battle field:
One with narrow corridors

upon coming into her senses,

it was
Momo.
