Through the long journey...
This coastal drive race
I swim through the void inside of me.

A gear shift, a dab on the brakes...
I lean my wheel to the left...
Tap the throttle through the turn

They are not human
So it does not matter what I do.

Closer and closer and closer...
They are artificial so they dont respond to racing maneuvers.

I brake later than the bot,
get the inside.
Block its racing line as I claim the apex.
Aim for the outside and get ready for the next corner.
Slowly applying the throttle...
This car is feisty...
Easy... easy... easy...
