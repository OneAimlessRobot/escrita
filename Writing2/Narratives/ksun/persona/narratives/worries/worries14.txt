Part 14:
the ascent and the fall.

So,
A few months ago,
Ksun was scouted out
by a french racing team,
from a small,
grassroots touring racing league she was competing at before.
Ksun used to race for the Autobacs works team
piloting in the 1400cc category
behind the wheel of a small Ford Escord.
It had a boxy body,
Minimal aerodynamic devices,
Apart from a small body kit 
that gets fitted to it in tuning shops owned by the aforementioned works team,
necesary so the car can make weight.
It featured a simple livery
consisting of a white background,
some orange and blue stripes,
An Autobacs banner at the  upper edge of the windshield
Ford logos on either side panel
and a valvoline symbol at the hood.

It featured a tried and tested in-line 4 cylinder  block made by cosworth as a base
that was then retrofitted with a single turbocharger
from the scarce selection that was actually allowed by the regulations,
and tuned to produce no more than 500 millibar of boost at its peak.

This setup,
alongside a dry sump
and the use of Castrol Motor oil culmimated in the production of around two hundred and eighty horsepower.

The dry weight of this setup was around 
one thousand kilograms,
the minimal weight for any competing vehicle
according to the regulations.

The teams name:
Was Peugeot... 
Total Energie. A factory backed endurance racing team that was now trying to expand into the sports car market,
beginning by promoting their brand-new road-going version of the Peugeot 206 T16 Pikes peak machine that set a certain American Road Ablaze back in 2013.
It was going to be called...
the S16,
as for "Sport"
And they were going to promote it by competing in the GT3 category.
With racing machines that were,
essentially,
Minimally regularized, spec-detuned versions of the real deal.
