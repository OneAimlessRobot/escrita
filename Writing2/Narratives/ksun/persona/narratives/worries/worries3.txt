Part 3: Flesh eater

Flesh eating tape worm
Lukewarm stimulation
Identity delusion
Contagious confusion
Mind altering protrusion.

Flesh eating street drug... 
is what I am
Kaleidoscope of facts.
Facts are still
Reality moves
the words I spell out
threading on grooves


What matters is that I am alive, right?

As I leave the highway...
What approaches is a series of uphil hairpins.
The mountain side.
The slithering rally these class B sports cars will have to climb.
There goes Aston, then the lambo 
and then me in a TVR now alongside a modified audi-tt

Wheels spinning.
Gearshifter clicking
Engine singing.

 At the exit...
A beautiful view of the natural haze-coated tropical forests.
Temperate isolation.
Vegetative swarming... 
is what I do if you let me in

You use chrome,
I use Tor.
Not the same.
We are different.
Im on seven watchlists.
You avoid seven sins?
I invent new ones!

I dig holes into the flesh of society...
Living from its nutrients.

I suck the economy dry
with the captivating event horizon
That is my soul.

the vacum in my body
Big like Betelgeuse
Bright like Rigel

Turning it into molten nickel.

heating up the prize
the forbidden fruit that i seize to survive
Stay alive
Stay sharp
Stay moving
