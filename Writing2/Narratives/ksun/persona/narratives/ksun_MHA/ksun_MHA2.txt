Imagine her after breaking the Dorodango ball:
at the mirror...

Disheveled...  

In the bathroom.  

her eyes are warm  

But hollow.  

lips are still colored red.  

But they wont curve
no matter what she does.  

She is not sure about how she should feel  

the heterochromia eyes 
ooze a certain resignation with the present

her shoulders and body language neutral.  

Like she is just standing before the mirror like an old friend.  

"So... we meet again.  
In the boys bathroom...  
As we run from them again, huh?"
