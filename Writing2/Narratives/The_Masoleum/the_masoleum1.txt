Title: "Pantry"
by Maria Henriques:



"
"Maria"...?
No.
Youre mistaken.
That
...
is not my name

because I knew Maria.
and...
she died in her teens
and is watching us from the clouds


i need to leave this place
please help me.
...
Maria is not here, anymore
but whatever I carry wrapped around me
people still need that


there is nothing to see.
just air.
air that my body cycles through
and slowly depletes


the air is inside
its cold
and its dry.
it gives my organs wrinkles
and the shapes I see become grey
indistinctive blurrs


i wish I had more
to say
but I dont.
so...


...
the inside of the school…
It is golden during the sunset.
it reminds me of those evenings at the health center
carrying around a stand
with a bag hoisted on it.
and a tube inside my belly


i disappeared slowly
it took years
but then...
came january
it was 2008
and my little sister had come back from school
and brought left overs from the cafeteria
all for me to eat.
It felt...
sinful.
I was so ashamed.
lugging around the death of my tissue
gorging on my blood and my bones
growing slowly
but growing heavier with every day that passed.
and they chose to give the food
to someone who was doomed.

...

and it might have been enough
...
That is the worst part.
it was enough for me to get through chemo
…
I will never repay that.
They gave me their food.
and I gave them whimpers
and sobs.

i was weak
and that...
guilt.
It made me a new woman.
Maria was dead.
I was now Mara
the corpse lady

"

...
I'm sorry.

and until next time
