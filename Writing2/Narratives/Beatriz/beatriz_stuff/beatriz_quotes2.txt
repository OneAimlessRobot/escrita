Lexa pro doesnt work:
Short poem:

Handle with care
Taken twice today
One in the morning
One in the evening
For ages
unable to feel myself breathing

Staying alive
rotten inside
Baking and shipping
cookies of rage and despair
My soul gasping for air
Some relief, 
Why not someone to touch
why not something to believe

Dispose of these packages
at the nearest pharmacy
so then
what happens to my body?
Fill with pills...
Decay and bury any chills

Flames of dissociation
come in surges
Unable to stop them
I surrender to urges

Unknown to most
Mr Muffler is a friend
Gifting you plenty of bliss...
of release...
Slumping grown Men
at one shot a piece
A defiant piece to your resistance...

Resistance
To my goals
next year...
and after
Another blown fuse
An alley filled with my laughter
