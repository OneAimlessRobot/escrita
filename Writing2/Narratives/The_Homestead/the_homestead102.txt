It starts with a lonely electronic guitar
Muttering to itself
Tasting the saliva in its mouth
And feeling the moment.
It trudges,
Tickles through chords
Sprinkling in notes and riffs
Quiet in its volume
But clear in its presence.
It then explodes
Because everyone arrives.
The band is now playing with all of its members
And the crowd
It listens attentively
To the sounds of kinship
Of an united group of people
Who appreciate the intricacies
of the summer time anxiety.
There is so much to do,
So much to see
Places to be
Everyone feels it too
But together
We make it memorable
Together we share our stories.
Even if no one speaks
Everyone hears each other's breathing
The pulse in our hearts
Drumming to the sound of cicadas in the backstage
Crickets hiding in the weeds
And ladybugs
Flaring their wings in this afternoon concert.
The crowd cheered when the band was complete
It clicks into place:
The dry heat,
The hot asphalt beneath our naked feet
And the hot concrete
That quietly absorbs the sun
We are all here in this put-aside small town
The small group of young adults
Like a hivemind that obeys the spectacle
We are much stronger as a set
The atmosphere protects us from heartbreak
And the community
Gives the mind
A reason to believe in life.
Sports,
Concerts...
Parties
And riots.
Everyone has a reason to gather
The reason does not matter
We all do it.
We need each other to feel alive
Or we eventually
Get fed up of ourselves.
The coastal water is salty
With the tears we all cried
While grieving the feeling of belonging
Before gathering in this packed arena
To watch our feelings get turned into sound
By the mages of emotion
The musicians we relate to
...
Because the universe
Is a crowd
And the crowd protects the lonely.
...
It may be overwhelming,
Chaotic
And annoying
But if it means that life is livable
We will stay here
For we deserve to be better
We deserve to belong
So belong, my children
Dance to your hearts content
Because here
every move you make
Will have somebody call out for you.
"Can I join?"
They ask.
What do you say?
You say:
"Why yes!
By all means.
Are you my friend forever?"
"It does not matter, does it?
It is not a promise anyone can make.
Friends will always part ways
So that is why we enjoy the gathering
Because grief
Is the biggest reason
That people turn bad.
"Lets try our new shoes together
The sillier it looks
They louder we laugh.
And if we find something that fits,
We do not need to announce it.
We quietly wear it in our private lives
No one needs to know
Because what happens in the crowd
...
Stays in the crowd."
Stay healthy, people
Because when the right people find you,
You do not want the bruises from wear and tear
To seep through.
They ruin the mood
They make them pity.
keep them to yourself
...
And let the music
Do the healing."
