Order






I cant go back to who I was
Im getting too hold to hang on to the past
if I keep chewing on the same gum
it will become stale
and it already is.
the image of the past i have is beautiful
The past would feel
flattered to hear such things about itself.
as the curtains close on this chapter
and the heat dies down
as the flowers of youthful innocence wither
memory becomes sweeter
it ages like fine wine.
No.
it will be better to say
that most wine
cannot even hope to age like a memory
the longer that it lingers
the more tempting its flavour
and the more satisfied the consumer becomes.
we are such innocent creatures
hoping our houses of cards never fail.
but even with no wind
the cards themselves will wither
and become soggy
from the humidity.
the wind comes in occasional gusts
they serve to pull away what feels too good to be true.
the heat comes after
to make sure that we are resolute
and that we can live with little.
and the winter makes us focused
it excites us with its challenges
and the spring will be the time
when the newer parts of us will grow
so that we can face the day with renewed certainty.
we are hoarders of certainty
collectors of fact.
we eat what is right
and what isnt
is annoying and inconvenient.
the thing that I am certain
is that I am sad
i want there to be vigor in my imagination
zest and curiosity
but now it is densely fiscalized
and it is choking me out.
this writters block will be the death of me
i swear.
i wish I could at least learn something from it
because otherwise
finding myself unable to provie art to others
with these otherwise useless hands
is so achingly shameful.
im sorry
for I cannot provide ad nauseum.
i need rest
and to isolate myself
until nostalgia comes knocking
the engine is beat up
and the bolts are all loose
from vibration,
wear
and debris on all of the different pumps and fans...
and valves
and canisters.
it will be so dreadful
i just know it
to be without writing
and to do it
deliberately
is the ultimate salt for my wound
the disinfectant
the cure
which is why
I will perservere
and one day
I will find the oasis, once more
and my soul will get its voltage back
