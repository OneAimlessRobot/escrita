So.
I went to the doctor,
And found out that I missed a psychiatry appointment.
I remained sat for a moment
Before pulling my phone out
To call the health centre where these happen
As I feel
Like I usually get called 
To get carried there
In a non-emergency ambulance
...
But I dont recall getting called.
That made me feel like I had forgotten the appointment
Which I had no explanation for
As it was supposedly on the first of july.
I think I had went there,
...
But the reality
Is that it is in the system
And as long as It is in the system
They are right
And I am wrong.
As the minutes of not getting a reply ticked by,
They attempt to redirect my call to the health center proper.
...
And after three tries
Each one including waiting for about 10 minutes,
I give up,
Abandon the routine appointment,
Telling the doctor that it "is over".
I abandon the health centre I was in
But not before taking my 4 euro earbuds
And smashing them into a wall
And leaving.
...
You cannot forgive yourself for your failures
As it changes nothing.
Youre the only one to blame
And if youre the one who does not know,
It is always your fault.
I came back to my house,
Spat on the marble floor
In the hallway of my appartment
Sat down on my desk
And wrote this down.
