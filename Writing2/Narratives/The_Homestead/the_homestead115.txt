Title: "Futility/Multiplicity of thought"
Author: "Adriano Valente"
Body:
"
At some point,
emotions become too hard to digest.
They become cement which blocks the water
And clogs the pipe.
They crystalize
And make you sick whenever you try to get them out.
The cold feels unpleasant
And the heat feels...
Greasy
Digest
Digest
Digest
It sounds so...
Physical.
As if answering a million questions
made no difference
if the body does not feel right.
The mind is fed by the body
Which is merely informed
Not powered
By the former.
The thoughts drive the body
But the body provides the platform,
The leverage
And the feelings of belonging,
Passion,
Anything  that you can experience
Comes from the body.
But ive grown attached to thought
Because they give the illusion of sophistication
But it is not enough.
It isnt the world which leaves me disappointed
it is the thoughts
That stopped being able to elude me
And now I feel distant
In the middle
But so above.
The people try to reach me
But the sophistication keeps me away from them.
Like a helicopter parent,
It filters out whatever I do
Through its pretentious lens:
It says not to touch,
Not to feel,
Not to get hurt.
I havent felt hurt in ages, but...
I have not felt much of anything else, either.
The thought of being bored for the rest of my life
Does not seem like doom, anymore.
It feels like reality
Im sick of thinking
It makes me nauseous
And the tedium
Is existential
As unresolved answers pile up
Like soot in the bottom of the pan,
The soul becomes gradually more crestfallen
Despondent
And disillusioned.
"
