cranked out like notes in a box

the soul drains

to express myself?

everyone tells me that I think too much

a sash that wraps around me and it
Or them
Or something like that
But when it starts feeding fear
Instead of vigour
It is not love, it is just unresolved insecurity.
There is a purpose in life
Which is to just keep moving.
Life has itself as a purpose
Just like the universe is the only thing that contains all things
Including itself.
Life is the universe of philosophy.
You cannot map the purpose of life to anything

Go to a doctor.
You may be depressed.

No.

From the warmth,
