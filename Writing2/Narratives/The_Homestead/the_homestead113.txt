Title: "Murcielago"
Author: "Adriano Lopes"
Body: "
I am at the parking lot
And it is seven pm
19th of august
2010.
This is Lourinhã, Portugal
I walk idly through the  loosely drawn lines on tarmac
wondering if being aware
is what takes away the purpose
To even get out of bed at all.
Because I think that
If I am delusional
I am able to always give my own life meaning.
Which Is why I come to buy groceries
Every single day
Even when I dont need to.
Is it too hot?
Here comes some juice.
Is it too cold?
I get my food from town subsidy
But some warm carbonara doesnt hurt.
I am lactose intolerant
But when I need snacks,
I always come to the grocery store
For butter based cookies.
In the grocery store,
There is enough people inside
For me to feel like I belong
Specially from late august to early september
Which is when classes start
And a burst of satisfaction
Materializes in my gut
Whenever I come here.
The air is slightly fresher
But it is still somewhat warm at night.
There are a few cars parked here
But not many
As people have not yet come back from holyday.
I am the only one that does this
I am sure of it.
I cannot think of anybody else
Or even a type of person
That genuinely likes hanging out at the grocery store.
But people cannot say that I dwell on a man cave
And also that i do not go outside if I do this.
Plus.
If I manage
By sheer miraculous luck
To meet a highschool peer.
Yes.
A 24 year old young adult
That still remembers their highschool peers by name
Yes.
I am soft like that.
A total child.
A child.
A manchild.
A child that cares too much
And ignores too little.
Every detail of this town
Is engraved into my soul.
The windmills at the small patches of crop
That people have in their lawn
The middle school
The naval engineering museum
And the houses that are yet to be rented.
...
The housing crisis hits hardest nowadays.
I am now going home.
Feeling comfortable in my skin.
Not metaphorically.
But physically.
The air swirls around my thin
Exposed limbs in a way that refreshens them
But does not annoy.
It is slow
And my earbuds do not flail around.
Everything is stasis
A perfect backdrop
For the random noise
That the brain uses to fill the silence
That I ought to have gotten used to, by now.
I am an adult
But I still feel the need to entertain myself at all times
No matter the situation
Every moment is spent listening to music,
Coding,
Watching anime,
Writing...
Or complaining about how I do not have the motivation
To do either of those things.
Motivation is a disaster
And silence is a drought.
There is no middle ground for me to settle
And the world
In the middle of such ambiguous stasis
When I have to give my take
is always
In disorder
"
