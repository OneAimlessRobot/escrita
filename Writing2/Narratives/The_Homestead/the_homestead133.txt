Title: "Blackness"

okay so this is a small rant

ive always felt very inferiorized
because: pro footballers need 3 things to do the sport:
1- to want to play the sport
2- to have the talent (instinct/knack) to play the sport
3- be willing to put in plenty of the kind of work the sport requires (temperance)

and a footballer can suffer a lot for having two of those
but not having the other one:
there is wanting and having knack
but putting in little work
there is wanting and being willing to work
but not having the knack
but...
number one always feels like the "easy" one to obtain...
even if I feel like it helps with number 3 AND number two.
it makes you willing to express yourself through the sport and make breakthroughs when the existing prescriptions for work dont fit you
making your own prescriptions and that sort of thing.
like being good at a very narrow portion of football
and coming up with bizarre training drills for that type of thing
even when others think its weird...
but nowdays everyone just wants this stuff to be digestible and predictible
for some sort of ghost audience....
anyways:

wanting is a constant in my life.
but then
I either cant put in the work to go further
which may happen in my projects I think
or dont have the knack
which happens in college assignments
where I can only get by with 12s by the skin of my teeth
while feeling like me
as a person
and even as a creator, is worth much more than a 12
even when I dont care about making things deployable or readable to others.
I just believe like I am good, okay?
I dont need others to tell me that.
And I dont think Im some sort of cheater or rule breaker for that.
we were born in this world to be original.
i dont give a fuck if some boring nuclear engineer tells me that someday its gonna fail
and well
so do nuclear reactors!

you think that you convince people to use nuclear by telling them it wont fail?
No...?
you just tell them: "Listen
Petrol plants fail all the time.
you just dont see it
because it is less catastrophic.
But the sheer ammount of paperwork
and safeguards surrounding the process of managing and building the things is so rigorous and tedious
that failure for a reasonably trained person is practically impossible

but if you want electrification in vehicular applications to become reasonable
at least for small ones like passenger cars
(heavy duty stuff will always need diesel whether we like it or not
its just the best thing out there
and we have built society on the convenience and immediacy of diesel, so...)
we need to combine [that] with nuclear energy!
yes!
if it fails it will be spectacular. but humans have such an annoying fear of failure that it holds them back.
its not about playing god.
its about comitting to risks when we think it will improve our lives.


yea thats the rant

bye :D
