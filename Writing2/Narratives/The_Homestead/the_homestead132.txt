Title: "Lightness"

[...]

I dont really feel like talking, right now.
I feel sad.

Surely someone will read this, though, right?

???: "Dont ask those questions.
Just go on!
Did you not want to write some sort of motorsport story
to celebrate how Cynthoni's music makes you feel?"


Yea...
thats true!
i get motorsport fantasies all the time!
But I just dont get the will to actually sit down and do it.
Im too busy working on my server and such. By the way: Is the way in which I break lines annoying?
Because...
The alternative would be to stay on the same line.
My writing is very self directed. As making myself more conventional would expose me to criticism
That I am not ready to accept without something in me breaking. When I [do] read my own stuff, however, It feels jarring to have so many things on different lines.
Awww... My sadness is hardening again. Im trying to have a sincere moment. I almost went over the edge and cried. But high functioning depression wins again. I guess it will be another endless month going around in circles.
I even wonder why people put up with me. My writing is annoying to read
And all I do is write programs that nobody cares about. My superhero pack is undeployable, anyway
because all of the heroes and subsystems are so deeply tangled and integrated with each other that no one would even know where to begin to iterate on it. Im such an amateur. My writing sickens me...
I look in the mirror
and I guess my looks are... decent. But I dont see anything valuable behind my eyes. For the past 3 years, Ive just felt like Im an amateur trying to pass off as a pro. I struggled a lot with college, as I get into passionate personal projects that really nourish me! But then everyone just says I have no purpose. And... I dont have anything to say back at them to disagree
So... I guess I have to agree with them. Also: People get annoyed at my ellipses. They dont like them. But I write this way, okay? Its how I do it. I know that Im not needed and a pest. But please just let me feel good about my writing, okay? I literally have nothing left! My writing sucks, my programming is irrelevant, and If I actually become honest with myself
And accept that living just for me is enough, society will abandon me
and make me die of hunger and homeless.
Thanks a lot, guys. I dont really like being alive,
like,
in general because Im just playing cat and mouse with a gran turismo 3 demo
And my controller has shorts in 5 different places.
It is really difficult to do this while being expected to go even at the same speed as the others!
Let alone the demo... I am thankful for my optimism and naiivety
without those, I would have not been here for this long. Without my characters, which always kept me entertained, I would have cried much earlier ago
But hey. I wouldnt be as interesting to look at.
So I have to keep them. Im not okay
Its not alright. My brain... Like...
I wish I could just take it out and put in the one of a dead person. That way, I could just act normal or something...
I feel really self conscious when people look at my writing just to tell me it has typos. I know that typos make my writing less gross to read or look at
But it just makes something inside me break every time they do it. There must be some sort of explanation for me being like this There must be. But the things the pastor says make no sense. The things my supportive friends say make no sense. The things that my validating friends say make sense but they are just echoes of what I already concluded. And the things the devil says make no sense.
Why should I care about anything? People on the internet dont like me. People in real life dont understand me. I dont understand me.
But I know that something must be wrong.
Something must be wrong. I am all tied up as my pastor would say
In reference to people living bad lives.
I really miss college. But I get hung up on fulfilling personal projects and losing one obligation
makes the attrition and anxiety of catching up much much much worse. Im not remarkable in any way. Why do I expect anything of myself?
Why do I try to do things my own way? I know that im still trying to prove my trauma wrong. That im not some sore loser because they didnt get the smart type. That I can also do the things the smart types do.



Overall Im just clutched into this really nasty bear trap of having to "be smart" in some really ethereal sense
And it makes me feel so much dread
that I retreat right back into programming and writing
Even if other people think my writing is unpleasent and gross
and would rather see me from the back
(Walking away)...

I know someone will not feel this way about me.
But this wound is very real
And the high functioning depression is just making me run in circles without stopping. I know that my writing is annoying. But I dont know how to write, okay? So that is totally expected!
I was not even the best writer in my class...
The good girls always got 18s and 17s in Native Language class in highschool
and I never managed to feel even stable while studying it
let alone comfortable. The academic things just make me feel so much dread, in general. I know that I will never do it. I will never try it. I wont give up. That is a quality that I can at least attest to having. I stand tall. I used it as a password at one point. yea! thats something that can help...
I should use passwords of mentalities I want to embody. If I write them over and over and over again, I will eventually learn them. If I am slow in learning...
wait. But the pastor said that god wants us to come to Him [fast]. But... what if I am slow?
Is that bad? I am so sorry, dad. Im so sorry everyone for being so annoying and needy and whinny.
Im sorry for all of the pathetic mental gymnastics I make you guys put up with. But It cannot just be my fault! Like-- come on! Why does every bad thing that I do have to be just caused by me?
Well... that is a trick question. Because it does not!
That makes sense...

In college, having to catch up is just the worst feeling. The feeling of not being able to feel proud of anything. Always catching up. Always inferior... Its like the level of challenge was always well above my talent paygrade. Im a second rate computer science student
and I should have given up.
Well, that is not true. I learned a lot of things that I use in my hobby programming.

...

what did this ramble teach me?
Nothing in particular!

But I did have a lot to say, so...
Im sorry for making you put up with this. But actually feeling my sadness is good every once in a while. I guess it wouldnt make sense for it to come out all at once
As it is simply so much.
Thank you, Father. For allowing me to express myself this way.
Amen.
