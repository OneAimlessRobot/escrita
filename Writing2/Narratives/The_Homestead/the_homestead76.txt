Gear of Despondency

```

I live in a state
Of constant dread and anxiety
There is venom in the gauges
Demons in telemetry.

The train has derrailed
We are off to the trenches
We were already on the mud
but now we are sinking

The frying pan of expectation
has fried us to stone
Cold, rigid and linear
And fear running wild.

I feel so bankrupt, right now...
People enjoy what I do, but...
The juice that comes out
needs to come from my veins
With so many characters
I could run a small city.

The rythm does not land even for me
But sure,
We keep going
Sprinting into impeding doom
We rip the roots that keep us stuck
With the sweet poison of hope

The haters are all right
I create on top of names
the names I dont mention
They silently wait for my downfall
And,
Deep down,
So do I...

ksun said it all
We consume for convenience
And then waste potential.
that is what I do.
Writing these crap poems
Just hoping.
"Lob it ahead!
Make something happen!"
I learned nothing
since I dropped out
I couldnt handle the heat.
Maybe it was fear,
maybe I had no choice.
But I feel like I achieved nothing
Nothing of what I wanted.
There are small victories here and there.
I keep writing
I keep turining the same wheel.
Come on.
Give me power
A spark
Anything.
I never felt so creatively unavailable.
When I thought my ideas mattered,
I felt much better
It was much easier
Perhaps I should stop
repeating myself so much
...
...
The wheels refuse to spin
while I am at the wheel
The gear of despondency
Oiled with cobwebs and dirt,
im trying so hard to make something
But the something does not want to be made
...
Come on.
Go.
Please.
Any time, now.
Come on...
Please?
...
...
I guess that I really fell off...


```
