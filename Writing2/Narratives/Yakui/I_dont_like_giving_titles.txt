I dont like giving titles

because I dont know how to describe what I write.

it is not structured
it is not plain
it is jagged,
and I dont want to trim it down.

that may be because I dont feel real,
but...
man...
trying to write this, right now?
buried in so much isolation?
it feels like friction.

like derating.


As if ksun,
driving a toyota gt1 was barrelling down mulsane at La Sarthe,
the drag from the wind in early afternoon,
the skies speckled with clouds...
gusts of wind washing by the cockpit
hitting at the round windshield with full force.

she very much keeps it pinned, but...
the speed does not go up.
it actually...
it goes down!
...
as the engine howls in anger,
the large twin turbo V8 machine shoving more air into its mouth as it keeps coming...
