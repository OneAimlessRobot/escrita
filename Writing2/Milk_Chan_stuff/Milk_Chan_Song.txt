I like this song because:
1- its basically generic (Very very good... but still generic nontheless) "english, broke bloke rap" with very gritty, realistic themes. A foil for the eerie, alternative stuff people usually associate with her. But I think this is a refreshing and still authentic way to see it.
2- Its atmosphere is so "English bloke" that it hurts.
3- Its like a song she has on her laptop that she plays during small victories (like finding a combination of pills that produces slightly less pain.)
4- Its not Industrial or intense rock, which is fitting.
5- I learned it from a sports game (Fifa Street 2) which is a nice contrast to the grim themes often explored in the game.
6- it fits her natural pet peeves with her OCD, that constantly tries to convince her that her life is a battleground
