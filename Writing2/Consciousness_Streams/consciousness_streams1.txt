Title: "Let it die. It is dead tissue"







Half baked attachment
Lies told by my attachment
Games played on limited time
Programs made on a budget of joy
My life is grey, a blanket of delusion
Shed some light on it
You will find wounded pride
Put salt on it, and the tears will pour right out
Driven by fear and a touch of longing
Right now, I'm not sure
Of what will be of me
...
...
...
Three hours in my bedroom, three days without the sun
My skin has grown dry
From the thirst that is in my mind
I've joined the dark side when the walls fell down
Because all I had left
Was a vague sense of hope
I'm not on par with their concepts
I haven't passed a single test in my life
Studying is hard because learning hurts
But with my own brain I will solve it all

I left all my friends
And all my friends left me
All alone
With the sourness of my own soul
Since I was young, I just wish I could feel safe
But at 24, I still don't feel at ease
Am I unfit?
Or does it really never rain in the desert?
I've ruined everything
I've destroyed it all. Now my life
Is just made of excuses
My house of cards is full of cracks
The star is burning carbon
The building is tilting
And that crash will shake my world apart
I've eaten the reserves first
And then the left overs
I am all empty. I have nothing to say for myself

...

Waiting for a call, for a ray of sunshine
But the sky is always grey
The vessels in my skin have lost all color
My petty traumas are what give me purpose.
To reckon with what I am is all that I crave
But what if it reeks?
Will I have to erase myself?
I crave presence and my family is worried.
But I can't go back.
Not until I fix myself
My friends have seen me broken
Now they must see me fine. I can't go back
And tell them nothing changed.
I spin in the same turn table
Playing the same songs
Because the truth is
I never learn anything alone
So please excuse me. Please be patient.
I'm not that smart. And specially not that strong
You overrate me! I waste your apples
I trashed your food. And made myself a rotten pie
I'm not a good cook. I'm a terrible one. Im sorry for the things Ive said
I've said them too many times.
This sorrow flows inside me of a life that remains still
The river is full of sludge, the acidity of my vapid thoughts.
I've filled up a bucket, but the house is still flooded
Full of desires. And things I can never reach.
Im powerless inside my world
Powerless inside my house. My inertia has grown limbs.
I'm powerless inside my soul.


...
Im sorry for existing
With this cloud of smog that follows me
Wherever I go. No one feels safe around me
I am still that weird kid
that everyone avoids. the one with incomprehensible demands
Who also won't talk
I've tried talking but the words mean nothing
I've tried acting. But just make everything worse.
My computer completes its duty
And the duty is my desire
My desire is my incompleteness.
The void of growing spoiled
The vanity of being good
And the illusion of diligence.
Im sorry for existing
With this burden to prove myself
You reassure me. But I still do not know myself
I need a cure for my neurosis
Is it to start over? And witness my body survive?
Is it to erase myself? And witness my identity bloom?
I know that it will hurt. You dont need to tell me.
I know Ive done wrong. The consequence is insoluble guilt.
I know that poetry does not solve anything.
But I dont know what else to do. You just told me what I did
How should I reply? Will it be wrong?
When I admit defeat...

...
The cadence I follow is my chaotic whim
The words I write are my diseased blood.
This disheveled song of undigested sadness
Is my tribute to what I have lived.

I'm leaving soon. My days are counting down
I just hope I could bring some happiness
To the ones that cared without me noticing.
Im so sorry for existing
With this stubborn wall I have built
The cover for my unfulfilled duties
The cracks under the surface
That I never felt allowed to patch.

When i was in college,
I had failed so miserably
That the ball had left the stadium.
I never had the courage to leave the stadium
Where I was being cheered and admired
To go look outside. because If I had broken a window,
Only god knows how hard she shame would hit my fragile self concept.
Always thrown about by minds that regiment my life
Decide my future. Im passive against my brother, my father
The brothers of my father...
The family of my father was so much better than that of my mother.
My mother gave me food. But that is the obligation.
My father wouldnt have ever taken me to school
Or made me study hard. But he would give me something to believe in
Something to acquire. A job to do. But now all I have is a broken puzzle
That Im subconsciously forcing everyone else to assemble for me.
The picture is what I look like
It is what I dont know.
What do I look like? God knows. But I dont
I tried playing god and ended up way down in hell.
Who is going to pay for that window?
How much will it take out of my earnings? I dont know
And I wont need to if I never leave
