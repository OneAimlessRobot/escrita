gravity_defying1


Man, five years is way too much time. It's way too much time. 
The way life just...
changes...
its haunting.
Its grief inducing



The wheels of the bus go round and round, as the trees that I see disappear before me. 


Clouds go away and away, as my feelings of grief carry me astray.


Disease and paradise, they all mingle into a single shade of blue.

For others,
certainty is safety
For me,
It feels like manifesting a new cage to dwell on

a manifestation  impatience.

A run from impermanence



At some point, life stops being fun

and begins turning into an oppressive cage

with nowhere for you to run.


I grieve having become a weapon

Now too heavy for ones tear-stained hands to wield

Within familiar spaces
I see strange, new faces
Roam around today,
tomorrow.

reminding me that being alone
is a universal kind...
of sorrow



Im not afraid of screaming.
Im just afraid of being heard



The coping is only bad
When surviving is confused for thriving
