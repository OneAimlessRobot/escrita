cloud_needles1

I need to make this real.
No time to mope about
No way to turn around
In the night,
Post nightmare
My beating
Anxious heart
Wakes my neighbour
From it sound!
3 in the morning,
Drenched in sweat
Eyes red
claw nails
shallow breath

Needles in my arm pierce deep into nothing
Staying awake for days
Because for me
only art feels like something

Juggling grenades
Dodging bombs 
Throwing shrapnel at the wall
Getting high from godhood
Until life shakes me apart into withdrawal

Paper cuts from isolation
Blast bunker in my soul
because I swapped 
tenderness 
for soft self detonation

Juggling bombs
Dodging spears
Bombing cities of my trauma
Destroying bunkers of repression
making my emotions
into noisy drama

Hurting from affection
Craving their rejection
Left abandoned in my cave
Stuck with my reflection.

Masking pain with mascara
Masking tears with laughter

because

tiny bits of plastic
Now live in my bloodstream
being thrown into jail
No longer feels like a dream

Sharp like a scalpel
Soft like snow
Cloud of needles within
Flowing like a typhoon

Shredding my integrity
Into bureocratic confetti
Performance anxiety
is my way of life

Angry from the nightmare
Exhausted from dripping 
blood into the toilet
The same one
that spawns 
needles of confusion

Swirling around 
Grinding me slowly
into a powder of confusion
These cloud needles
they feel safe
they enable the delusion

Hate does not begin
To describe what I feel
Because I know
That those emotions
Are not real

Disappointed others
Disappointed myself
I exchanged a rotten house
For a broken shell

So now it is time 
to really take it out
Bodies everywhere
Is what needles of despair
Leave behind
After leaving their cloud

Cyclonic winds of emo poetry
They dance inside my skull.
Blades, scalpels, 
spears of blood inside my guts

Juggling thoughts 
and emotions like a pro
Bro
My art leaves dents
It just doesnt pay rent
Each poem I release
Trampling my trauma into catharsis

Cloud needles of need
Injection of love and anxiety
Disorganized attachment
Is part of my design

Blood, poetry, bodies and minds
Juggled inside my head
Swirling inside
Storm of despair
like a tornado of flashy flair
Swirling around me
Craving love that is fair
