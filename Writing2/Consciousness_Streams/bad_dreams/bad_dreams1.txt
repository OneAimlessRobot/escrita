Today...
I woke up after a nightmare,
but then slept like 3 more hours
after taking my medications.
So...
I am okay now.

I dont wanna rehearse it.
I dont like rehearsing dreams.

But...
There is an unresolved detail there:
There was this girl in highschool, right?
So...
about 5 years ago.
She was your typical obedient,
Low profile girl that just liked school, right?
She helped her grand mother back at the farm,
As she was from rural origin.
She was smart,
She was athletic,
Even if she did struggle a bit with abstract thought
or in caring about philosophical or human things.
She was kind,
Selfless... even if she did come across as cold, sometimes.

But.
In my nightmare,
there was a gathering with me,
her,
And a teacher plus some other students.

I was the last to arrive
And they were all sitting on the ground, right?
the ground was cold, grey, slick, but polished and cement.

I was asked to sit at some place.
the problem?
For some reason,
Every time I walked over to that spot to sit,
My mind began dissociating,
My conscience always beginning to leave my body the moment I stayed there.

So...
Then I complained:
"hey...
Uhmmnnn...
I cannot sit there, Mrs... [it was a she]"
Her: "Why?"
Me: "because....
Umhn.,..
When I sit there,
I dissociate and stuff..."


To which he girl replied by saying: "But...
That is not a problem.
Just sit where you are told like everyone else!
I also do not like waking up at 6 in the morning to help my grandmother at the farm,
But always do it anyway.
And I dont need any "dissociation" as an excuse to stop doing it,
Even if doing it every day is annoying"


After which I woke up.
...
Sweaty...
Groggy, and wounded.
