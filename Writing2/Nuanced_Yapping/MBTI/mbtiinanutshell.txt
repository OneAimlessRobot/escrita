What mbti actually is:
- NOT a science
- It is a self-contained, 'a priori', conceptual reasoning based FRAMEWORK of understanding different ways (16 of them) the information flows from the subconscious to the mask and the sorts of ways people act on that information. It is NOT empirical science. You cannot figure out someone's type if you dont have previous insight from someone who exhibits characteristics from someone who knows their own type (that can be the same person)

Socratic metaphor: "Having an osciloscope does not make you an electrical engineer. You need to know how electricity works first"
