I have a music streamer that is a distributed system. Its organized in an index. Wait... I came up with a solution... but let me explain: the issue is that... in the stuff I was doing, I was only using one. And organized it like a tree where there is one Master server the server browser acesses so clients know what servers they can acess. The network is composed of client, servers, heartbeat servers and master servers. I know its dumb. The instructor I showed it to told me it was dumb. But anyways. The clients are the giraffes that eat the leaves, the servers. The servers are connected to heartbeat servers, that keep them alive as long as they can reply back with an acknowledgement udp continuous transaction. They talk back to either the main master servers or other heartbeat servers. The server browser can consult the children and the parent of these nodes... until the client, through their browsing, finds an actual server. They can then try to connect... ok but thats beside the point: the bad thing about this is that, wherever it fails, everything a level "below it" goes *poof*. My instructor told me I could not just have one index: I had to have multiple... and had to make them acknowledge eachother.
Decentralize them, essentially.
And... Im currently trying to cook out the specifics of how I will do it.

Ive also cooked up a way to make the server regenerate in a non-catastrophic way in case of failure. Instead of making everyone die .. the children will just try to connect to the master of the master, which is easy to make them keep track of...
Thats basically it.
