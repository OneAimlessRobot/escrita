A model that explains a meta micro strategy for behavior changing

alright...
I am not sure If I am ready to articulate this in a way that I feel would do it the justice it needs...
But here we are:
So...

Behavioural change in clinical treatment and pattern tracking done within a session with the guidance of a psychiatrist or within our minds through introspective sessions.
In the latter one...
Which is where 95% of the work happens...
Patients often find themselves lost in how to propelly deal with in-the-moment strategy-implementation failure...
As they quickly feel like they are completly lost in the fog
As the understanding and proper context framing for why the aformentioned strategy 
Is useful and when...
is the only thing that was, 
in practice, 
given to them right before they were, once again...
released into the wild...
in theory... fully ready to tackle that one reduced and achievable goal.
But... even achievable and small goals are not guaranteed victories...
And the patient might still fail to implement stategies...
Such as:
"having a particularly bad day and then suddenly finding themselves 
"just checking out" 
the social media profiles of their toxic ex. romantic/Friendship/workmate partner...
Which then becomes:
1- validation seeking through scrolling
2- several ugly things that are not relevant here because...

the point I am about to place on the table is:

Failure *Will* happen!
Whether big... small... 
macrogalactic...
or even microscopic!
its human!
and it happens to everyone!

I will start this micro strategy with a familiar example for me:

OCD detail-oriented-perfectionistic spiraling:

Imagine that you just finished your college assignment.
You poured alot of yourself into it...
But then imagine that the source of your OCD is a deep identification with how well you perform...
And then you find yourself submiting...
And checking out each sentence of your essay... one by one,
once again...
And then sending again...
And doing this repeatedly...
Over...
And over...
And over again...
Until the monster "is satisfied".

You stop after you do it for the 10th time...
After you just consumed about 60% of the potential creative energy and emotional bandwidth you had before the this localized onset of this particular pattern.
"And now I wont even enjoy my food...
My friends will hate me because I am a mess...
I hate me...
And I am broken....
And yatta... yatta... yatta."

And then?
this cycle of moment-to-moment reaction to perceived failure before self-imposed standards of "neat and efficient" healing become self fulfilling prophecies....
Leading to internalized beliefes that will make the person suffer way more friction...
Anguish... and shame throughout the healing process...
Which is counter productive...
because the first thing the therapist will try and do...
After they lay the payload on the patient...
is soothe their shame...
So they come back to the office...
Ashamed...
Over...
And over...
And over again...
Leading to an internal sense of dissatisfaction...
Hopelessness...
Financial insecurity because...
Joking aside... Therapy is NOT cheap on this day and age...
And that will make them spiral EVEN HARDER- BUT....
But...
Lets just slow down.
Okay?

Lets frame this differently:


And Im even going to make this *really* visual
Lets frame this like you are a string of infantry divisions under the leadership of country A...
Trying to stop an invasion from country B.
Lets assume that these divisions can only engage with full-contact either:
body-to-body...
Or melee-weapon-based combat...
Just for the sake of simplicity.
Army B is trying to halt away the advancing of army A.
Imagine that the patient is still at a point where they are trying to *Just*.
*not*
*lose*
territory.
(Imagine that this is the first time in their lives they ever got diagnosed...
And that they are like...
A young 20-25 year old young adult...
Maybe they have left college behind
Due to the excessive expectations they were enforcing on themselves
Which was slowing down the healing to a crawl...
And are now just surviving on some low-effort job...
Healing with creativity or sports or whatever...
Or videogame- you name it.
basically using the money they get from said job for a limited selection of things,
Such as:
therapy.
Bills.
food.
small extras (Shoes ripped...
Shirt ripped... 
Pc needs a new cooling fan...
stuff like that)

What you do is...
You use the extra allocated emotional badwidth to...
*prehemptively*
emotionally antecipate possible conflicts.
Because...
right now?
their army is just fighting with tunnel vision and myopia.
They need a backup plan for when they *get defeated*.
Engineers *plan for failure*.
(This is also the whole crux of what OCD lacks in their lives, specifically... but I digress.
This is also somewhat applicable with rather comfortable ease to things such as:
1- Psychotic outbreaks that have escaped containment from taught grounding strategies...
2- Someone who is autistic yet brilliant shelling up to their comfortable interests after failing to separate their identity from the information they interact wuth...
etc.. etc... etc...
Anyways)
So... one thing they have to think about is...
*Assuming they fail*...
They do *not* think on missed out success...
Because... maybe it was not entirely their fault that they failed...
Or... at least...
its good to practice trying not to jump to such conclusions reactively...
And actually think them through
because...
if you fail? You fail! there is no grey area!
let go! Think ahead!
"Ok... so the army have taken two main cities...
they are clearly fast and gaining momentum...
We cannot keep clashing head on.
Lets retreat more into the inland and form barricades in cities with a healthy ammount of supplies.
The lost lives do not matter...
There are no respawning devices in the real world.
Lets try not to lose the remaining ones.
the emotional bandwidth 
that the OCD army occupies on their way to the actual fortifications is not relevant.
That time that you consume mourning your loss is spent...
but it is also neither...
Wasted...
Or gained.
Its just a reaction.
Just focus on fortifying the layer below the one they have already captured...
And as you retreat and let yourself go...
do NOT stop to look back...
Do NOT overthink.
Just run... deeper and deeper into your essence...
And find some place where you can find some stronger fortification.
You do NOT need to win EASLY.
You only need to either WIN...
Or go back home... still alive.


And yeah...
Thats a micro strategy that I came up with.
