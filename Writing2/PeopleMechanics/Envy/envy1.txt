Envy pt1
Do you want to know which of my characters embodies envy? Ester. Because she's just so obsessed with having stuff that other people have. She wants Filippa's strength. She wants the religious girl's innocence. She wants this. She wants that. She wants this. She wants that. But, because she wants absolutely everything, she gets absolutely nothing in return. The event queue just clogs. The machine just overheats and spirals.
