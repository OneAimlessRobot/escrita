fearmechanics1


Everyone has like...
Fear of heights right?
Because I think there is an important distinction to be made between:
1- phobic fear (i know its redundant wording but... yeah...)
2- survival reflex reactions.
Lets take heights as an example:
If...
being aware that you are in a high place...
And that, for the sake of the example, Even being [inside] of a skyscraper apartment, inside a bathroom of a friend's house where, in theory, there should be no windows or any visual indication of how high above level ground you actually are...
But [just] having the thought fills you with existencial dread,
Full cowling anxiety 
and various symptoms similar to that of a panic attack?
Like quick breathing,
Sharply increased levels of stress
Sweating...
And jagged voice delivery?
And you just cannot shake that off on your own and need someone to ground you and do breathing exercises with you so you calm down?
And if it can go away with a joke or a jump scare?
[Then...]
Its [phobic fear]
its unnatural, highly painful,
Severely cripples day to day functioning
And you should seek help,
Because that is not how you are supposed to react to [just the thought] of being high up.

BUT...

If you are just chilling in your bedroom...
And lets say that its the first time you have ever been to a sky scrapper...
You have always lived low to the ground and maybe never lived in a building with a balcony...
And decide to go to said balcony...
And take a peek down...
And your body has an instant reaction to the height like: "Buah-- Thats tall!"
And your first instinct is to go back inside (or look away from the ground below)
And the sensation goes away immediately and you are able to focus in whatever you were doing before with no problem?
Then thats a survival reflex.
And its perfectly natural if you are not used to high places...
And this one is the same with snakes...
If you are not used to being around snakes, 
you will obviously not like being near them very much
(for obvious reasons)
Its the same with bugs
That evolution
(And the medieval ages)
(And news articles about mosquitoes and stuff)
(And spider "myth sharing")
Taught us to avoid naturally
As they are natural predators and being near them 
causes the primitive part of our brains to react accordingly.

So...
If you can look down that balcony...
And not get a survival reaction,
You are either:
1- Clinically unafraid (I dont know if it has a name... 
but it ought to be the result of a serious condition, I think)
2- You have trained yourself and are a badass. Congrats :)
3- Are really brave/emotionally done.
