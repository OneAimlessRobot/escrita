I think creativity is both an innate skill and a trained one. 
For me, personally, its all about figuring out how you work and the specific things in your life that make you flow. Its not just random bursts of wisdom or insight. These things can be trained and grinded for. Its a train that, at first, only seems to appear every other month so... you just wait. You feel that all you can do is wait. But then you notice. There isnt just one train line. There is one right behind you too. So now you keep in touch with both. But then you notice... there is one above you. And one below... there is one beyond the first one...
Its all about keeping an open mind
Not being cynical.
Conditioning your body to make the trains always appear at the same times.
Instead of chasing storms
You will start to brew them
You will start hunting for them.
When you can catch a big fish...
And feed yourself from it...
Thats when you can start thinking about selling to others.
Your writing should serve others, but it should serve you first and foremost.
And anything can be an outlet for creativity.
Shawn O'Malley has fighting.
Hartem Ben Arfa has football
Elon Musk has Business.
I have programming and writing

Anything that your creative fluids fit seamlessly in
Anything for which you have enough fluid to completely fill
Anything for which you have enough hunger to be willing to fully dedicate your life towards.
Its like love...
But performative.
Its like fishing..
But with ideas.
But you should not let it consume you.
Or you will eventually become it.
And then...
The millions of trains will become disorienting
And you will lose yourself in the fog.
So you need talent BUT...
You need skill
You need hunger
You need goals
And you need discipline.

Overall...

You need vitality

And never, ever stops
