Do you know a good, simple and immediate exercise people can do to begin expressing themselves more fluently and coherently?
Use more of what we call in portuguese grammar, "Orações subordinadas"
And not so many "Orações coordenadas"
Ou "Orações simples sem conjunção".
Sentences with expressions like "Because", "However", "In fact". Because these are words that connect ideas together and allow people to tidy up their reasoning a bit more and be more clear to anyone who is listening.
Its also good for traumatized/neurodivergent people, as they tend to speak more in symbolic fragments than fluid sentences.
For example:
I had a friend who was basically sharing her lore with me (She... went through stuff)
And she spoke in a way that resembles someone during a panic.
She would utter a fact or event, and then juat cut to the next.
Utter another...
And just cut again.
Her internal logic and justifications for her actions made sense, though...
Its just that she spoke in this way: "This happened. And then X happened. And then Y happened. And then... and then... and immediatly after... and right before".
Its kind of hard to follow for someone who isnt Neurodivergent.
And it makes it harder to put the puzzle of your past together.
