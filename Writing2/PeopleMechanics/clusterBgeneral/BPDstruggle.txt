



BPD people want connection.
Their void is clear communication and boundaries that they never got to assert...
Like a defective  TCP socket
They experience life where their favourite person at the time is like their favourite video
And they are forced to watch the same video...
over... and over... and over again
With lag spikes,
A 1% connection strength
And constant buffering... 
stuttering... 
Interruptions...
And server failures.
It makes you dead inside...
Impatient...
As if you get hungrier and hungrier for packets...
And the more you ask,
the less that come back to you.
