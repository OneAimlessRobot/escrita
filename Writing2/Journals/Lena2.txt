Lena journal 2:

"How am I?" you ask?
I am a pretentious
Hairless monkey that fell out of its tree when trying to destroy the world... 
I feature several organs... 
they... work... 
there are also several fluids circulating all around my body, 
where they serve diverse functions.
I also feature hair.
I also feature two hands.
Each boasting 5 fingers 
and an opposing thumb
that I can use to manipulate small or big objects
And perform complex physical tasks.
I also feature a spinal chord that allows my skeleton, 
and therefore the remaining of my frame, to maintain a well defined structure during locomotion
