maria henriques journal


I am the taxi service of ghosts

No...
Im not pulling some kind of trick.
Spirits from the Defunct live in my body
they greet me in the morning
and leave with farewells in the evening.
after that,
There is nothing
just the sound of my slow yet restless breathing
and the old friends that I am still waiting for


My life is heat dissipation on auto pilot
exertion that erases all doubt
only leaving behind
a vague sende of pride
and exhaustion


is it not... pain what I am describing.
it is the shivering breeze that comes directly from this cavity
agape inside of me
what was left of my former self is no more


ive been a bad girl.
so rude.
so irritated
so impatient.
my reflection shows me the back of my head before I go to sleep.
Even I would rather see myself
from the back


I am the crux of it all

but lets not talk, for now.
I need to stop for today.
the ghosts as well.
Tomorrow, we will do it all over again
