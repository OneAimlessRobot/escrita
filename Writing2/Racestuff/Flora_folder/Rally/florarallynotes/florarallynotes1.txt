Flora in Rally?

Flora in HillClimb?

Just imagine the car.
Her monster Gillet Vertigo with 4 turbochargers.
She is going through a tight road cutting through a remote norwegian forest
adjacent to a rural cozy village 
full of cafés 
and sensible people camping on the edges of the track 
and taking photos of the event with their phones.
She is accelerating.
She yanks the sequential dogtooth gearbox lever
1st gear
*Click*
2nd gear
*Click*
3rd gear
140 kilometers per hour
approaching a hairpin
The miniature block being possessed by hurricane levels of forced induction
The turbochargers wheezing and stuttering through every drop of throttle.
The leaves being cast upwards behind the sea of turbulent air released by the beast's
Huge underside rear diffuser.
braking,
*Click* *Click* *Click*
She likes to brake deep and fast...
Making use of her dexterity with the clutch pedal as she downshifts like a machinegun
she flings the lightweight vehicular gremlin
around the hairpin
she accelerates again
in 2 seconds she reaches 80 kilometers per hour
passes by some spectators as she weaves through some winding turns
then dives back into the forest,
You can hear the ultra-loud turbo sounds coming from the miniature V6 that's being force-fed tons and tons of air
Suddently appearing like a jumpscare...
popping
banging
wheezing like a possessed demon clatter
leaving behind high pitched transmission whine
and one hell of a good day for every spectator that...
that is able to travel and witness this event honestly...
