If I had to summarise GirlB
As an engine tone,
or sound,
It would be...
A tubular,
Echoing groan.
Hollow
With a soothing undertone.
It would sound blunt,
But patient.
Big.
Not in volume,
but in a way
That echoing sound feels big.
...
Akin to some independently built
british made
V8/V6 sports car
From the 90s or early 2000s.
It would have a curvy,
but simple sillouette,
A coupe body
And the finish would resemble
A worn out,
Dusty plastic.
It would remind me
Of a playground slide,
That kind of material,
Except it would vibrate
At 6 to 7 thousand RPM.
